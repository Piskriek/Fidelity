# Pre-registration: bridge v1b, live against yoked (2026-11-02)

This file is written before any counted v1b run. Its hash goes into `RUNLOG_bridge.txt` with the hashes of the runner, scorer and worker. It is the second stage of BRIDGE_DESIGN.md §6.7's `PREREG_bridge_v1.md`, after v1a (`PREREG_bridge_v1a.md` and `mind/runs/v1a/RESULTS_bridge_v1a.md`).

**Not blind.** It follows v1a's results, the design, and three 600-tick check runs on world 31000, which is not scored.

## The question

**E2, from its own timeline:** does a character whose private heads learn from its own answers (L) do better than one whose heads take the same learning steps on another character's answers (Y)?

## The yoked arm Y (§6.1)

- Y runs two lanes in lockstep on the same world seed and trunk.
  - The **source** is an exact L life: its own world copy, its own live heads, the same action-sampling stream as L.
  - The **actor** is Y's four characters, acting in their own world copy by the §4.4 rule, with their own private heads, on the same action-sampling stream.
- Each tick, actor character c takes one Adam step on its own heads, with the same rate, betas and clip as L. The loss is the ANSWER loss on the source's character (c + 1) mod 4.
  - It is computed through a shadow stream that reads that character's tokens through the frozen trunk, with the actor's own heads.
  - The actor never trains on its own answers. Its own stream only forwards, for its choices.
- The actor acts first each tick. So its heads hold the source's answers up to the tick before, just as L's heads hold its own answers up to the tick before.
- No help from another timeline on bridge 2, as for F and L.
- What Y's heads learn: each season, every fruit kind is shared by all four valleys with probability 1/2. Otherwise each valley draws its own effect, which matches a neighbour's by chance 1 time in 3. Season turns are world-wide.

## Checks

**Built into every scored Y record.**
- **K5** on the actor and on the source.
- The **source's NGE and collapses must equal the v1a L record** for the same model seed and world, which checks determinism. If they do not, the scorer refuses the Y record.

**Run before the lock** (600 ticks, world 31000, seed 1; `mind/runs/y_checks/`):
- K5 = 0 on actor and source;
- the source equals a plain L run of `live_y.mjs` exactly;
- with the head rate set to 0 (`--headlr 0`), Y equals F exactly, in NGE, answer CE and collapses.

## Readouts and rule

- **Readouts:** the primary readout is NGE, as in v1a. The secondary readouts are v1a's, plus the collapse causes, loop-breaks and end intensity that `live_y.mjs` records (the post-hoc diagnostic's tallies).
- **The rule** is v1a's (§6.4), with v1a's margin m = 1.02 (from v1a's F − F′) and v1a's regime checks M1–M3, recomputed. X is ahead of Y when all three hold:
  - (a) for each model seed, the mean of X − Y over its 16 worlds is > 0;
  - (b) at least 36 of the 48 pairs favour X;
  - (c) the mean of X − Y is at least m.
- **E2:** L ahead of Y.
- **Reported only:** Y against F (two-sided).
