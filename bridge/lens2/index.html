Four Valleys, live

192 lives running on the PC: frozen minds against minds that keep learning from their own answers. Every number here goes up when a character does better. Each little landscape is one character: its ridge is how sure it is of what the world will answer, and the sky turns with the seasons.

Four little characters live in four valleys full of fruit. Some fruit feeds them, some is poison, and which is which changes with the seasons without anyone telling them. We are watching 192 of their lives at once. Half the minds never learn after they are born; the other half keep learning from what happens to them. Higher is better everywhere on this page.
