## Q1. Does Ra tie abilities to kind or origin?

**A. Second-density kinds**
- **C08. Kinds named (19.2-19.4).** Three kinds of second-density being become self-aware: animals (most common), vegetables, especially a sound-vibration "tree", and minerals (rarest; a place becomes individual through love exchanged with a third-density entity). They are not enspirited from outside; awareness grows from within (19.3). On Earth they take human-like form (19.4).
- **C09. Telepathy belongs to the stage, not to a kind (19.5-19.6, 19.15).** Outside aid to second-density beings was telepathic only, "due to the nature" of second-density beings, sent by the Confederation. In 19.15 the second-density self includes the pack or tribe. Per-kind differences in how animals, trees or minerals communicate: silent in the sessions checked.
- **C10. Traits that follow the kind of heritage.** Tree heritage lowers bellicosity (38.9-38.10, above). Some second-density fauna have instinctive imprinting that gives monogamous mating, and third-density bodies arose from such beings, but free will is "far stronger than the rather mild carryover" (99.10). Varied physical vehicles are inherited from second-density forms (90.5). Only about 13 to 15 percent of known third-density forms would look similar enough to be called human, and Ra cites the varied capabilities of second-density life forms (90.10). Ra is unsure why this Logos chose the bipedal ape form and offers a supposition: speech over telepathy, and the thumb leading to tools, to intensify the veil (90.12).
- **C11. Individuals differ inside one kind (46.2).** Of two cats, Ra says one is harvestable third density and open to the same psychic attack as the group, while the other is less vulnerable for lack of mind-complex activity in the distortion of conscious devotion (paraphrase). Ra gives individual mind-complex activity as the reason, not the kind.

**B. Third-density natives of Earth**
- **C12. Many origins (9.6-9.8, 10.1, 10.3, 10.15, 19.5, 59.4-59.5, 6.13, 10.6, 91.13).** The first third-density people came from Mars, combined with careful genetic adjustments by the guardians, after Mars could no longer hold them (9.6-9.8). Some of Earth's own second-density beings graduated unaided, others with aid (19.5). Maldek's third-density consciousness took second-density physical forms that lacked the dexterity for third-density mental work (10.3). Mu beings were largely drawn from a second-density planet in Deneb; Atlantis formed as a conglomerate (10.15). Maldek supplied over two billion current souls and about 1.9 billion came from many places (59.5). Ra counts three major planetary influences and thirteen minor groups besides Earth's own second-density derivation, and calls the figure unimportant but harmless (91.13).
- **C13. Genetic lines and what they changed (18.18-18.21, 9.7, 64.14).** Ra gives one purpose for the genetic interventions of the Yahweh group, the first about 75,000 years ago: to develop traits that speed spiritual development, namely sharper sensitivity of the physical senses and a stronger mind complex for analysis (18.18-18.19). A later round, about 3,600 years ago (Ra corrected the date), was done with the Orion group and the Anak to make bodies larger and stronger (18.20). Ra says that attempt failed because the larger beings saw themselves as elite (18.21, paraphrase). The summaries I read disagree on who did what, and the page does not settle it. In some cases the 25-hour sleep cycle is due to Martian-race memory and the genetic material it gave to many bodies (64.14).
- **C14. Second-density-form groups on Earth (9.21, hedged).** The Maldek-origin group in second-density form is less able to be aware of the proximity of other complexes, and a reserve group escapes detection through technological understandings it had before incarnating (9.21). WebFetch flagged unclear referents, so this is not used for any label.
- **C15. Origin is told by biases, not looks (91.12, 91.7).** Some, not all, races and sub-races have different planetary origins, and Ra points to "biases concerning interactions with other-selves" and how the self is defined, not skin pigmentation. Transferred groups add to the planetary mind, and each race has its own racial mind (paraphrase).

**C. Harvestable entities from other planets**
- **C16. 63.9-63.17.** Fewer than 35,000 (63.12) harvestable third-density entities from other planets are incarnating with a "double body in activation" (63.13). The transitional body lets them take in fourth-density influx without disrupting the third-density body, which still dies on its normal schedule (63.13-63.14). They are partly conscious of fourth-density understandings that third density forgets (63.14). Earth is their fourth-density home, so they are not Wanderers (63.15), and only entities with strong service-to-others orientation earn this (63.15). Ra agrees that most metal-bending children are this type (63.16). Wanderers are third-density activated, are subject to the forgetting, and Ra says only disciplined meditation and work can penetrate it (63.17, paraphrase). The "fourth-density body in activation" wording of 63.17 is the questioner's, which Ra confirmed. Whether the children have the ability from birth: silent in the sessions checked (63.16 says only that they "demonstrate" it).
- **C17. Related (hedged).** 65.19: the newer fourth-density abilities some entities show come from present experience, not memory (my reading of an edited text; WebFetch said it could be read more narrowly). 66.7: dual-activated entities can use healing techniques "in many cases", and beginners of fourth density may lack the desire. 58.23: a visible gift such as bending is "a lesser signpost" than what is sensed in the entity, and indigo-ray contact need not show as any gift (paraphrase). 65.6: paranormal events are manifestations of entities whose vibratory configuration contacts the gateway to intelligent infinity, and whether they are used in service is free will.

**D. Wanderers**
- **C18. What they bring (65.11-65.12).** Each Wanderer adds to the planet's love and light and can serve as beacon or shepherd, and each also has "unique abilities, biases, and specialties" offered before incarnation. Examples: fifth-density Wanderers have great ability to express wisdom; fourth- and sixth-density Wanderers are immense passive radiators of love and light (paraphrase). Their help is not aimed ahead of time; light goes where it is sought (65.11).
- **C19. Brought in, but veiled (65.19, 66.6, 17.19, 12.28, 12.30, 63.17).** The forgetting has two reasons, the genetic link to the body and the free will of third density, and it can be penetrated far enough to recall identity and purpose (65.19). Ra compares a Wanderer to an infant who remembers speech but cannot yet produce it, and says a Wanderer's chance of healing is only somewhat above a native's, because of a possibly stronger desire to serve (66.6, paraphrase). Jesus learned the ability by "a natural kind of remembering" in early childhood and remembered more than most Wanderers (17.19). The largest group of Wanderers is sixth density (12.28) and Wanderers generally carry some handicap from the density gap (12.30).

**E. Healing and the inner planes**
- **C20. Healing is not tied to a planet or one density (17.18, 4.9, 4.11, 4.13, 4.14, 6.1, 66.8).** Healers can come from any density with spirit consciousness, third through seventh; the healer comes to know mind and body, then integrates spirit (17.18). Finding someone who brought the ability into the incarnation is the first step, and Ra confirms "This is correct" (4.11). Those who chose service may bring skills in "totally latent form" (4.9). Some heal without proper training (4.14), and a person who does not live the Law of One may still heal (4.13). A random gateway in the spirit complex (for example from LSD) taps energy without control, while a careful, conscious opening serves more dependably (6.1). A Wanderer first balances its own centers (66.8).
- **C21. Inner planes (17.36-17.38, 83.3, 86.10).** Entities occupy planes according to their vibrational nature, with seven astral and seven devachanic primary levels (17.36-17.37) and seven sub-planes of this space/time (17.38). Before the veil, dreams were used to learn and teach with inner-plane entities and with higher-density beings (83.3). Dream recall is a trained skill (86.10). Access to the inner planes tied to a kind or origin: silent in the sessions checked.

**F. Third density on other planets**
- **C22. Veil: only part is stated.** The veils are integral to the third-density choice in this Logos, and above third density there are no veils (77.14). For a case where only service-to-others polarity had evolved, Ra says the veil was not in effect (79.7, answer "No", question conditional). The first mind/body/spirit beings had no forgetting or veil (82.12). Veiling experiments began at about the same time in many galactic systems and the result spread through the octave (81.32-81.33, both confirmed), and various Logoi found the free-will pattern by different methods (81.30). Whether each third-density planet today has the veil: silent in the sessions checked. GUIDE synthesis: Ra presents the veil as a Logos-level design adopted widely, not as a trait of Earth alone.
- **C23. Before the veil, abilities were different, for all (79.32, 83.20, 83.3, 82.17-82.20, 86.18).** Magical potential in third and fourth density was far greater before the change, with much less desire to use it (79.32). A mind/body/spirit could control blood pressure, heartbeat and pain intensity, which later became involuntary (83.20). Pre-veil societies varied infinitely, many were technologically advanced because results came easily, and some traveled to other planets, but they lacked will (82.17-82.20; my two reads numbered these questions differently, so treat the exact numbers as approximate). Full knowledge of the body gave free choice but little polarity (86.18).
- **C24. Forms and communication elsewhere.** Third-density forms vary widely across the universe; about 5 percent would pass unnoticed among Earth's people (90.8) and 13 to 15 percent would look human enough (90.10); the third-density markers are self-consciousness, pairing, societal groups and races, and seeking meaning (90.9). Ra's own Venus third-density gives population and harvest figures (89.28) and harmony of interaction (30.14); neither says anything about communication. Mind-to-mind communication on other third-density planets: silent in the sessions checked.
- **C25. Mind-to-mind elsewhere in Ra.** Fourth-density negative groups use telepathy and transparency of thought at the top of the power structure (38.14). Ra says the least distortion in Confederation contact seems to be in mind-to-mind communication (8.31). The channel's natural telepathic ability is cyclical and its high point only sharpens reception (89.4-89.5).

## Q2. Present at birth, or opened by practice?

- **C26. Brought in.** Healing ability brought into the incarnation (4.11, 4.9). Wanderers' talents offered before incarnation (65.12). Jesus's ability by natural remembering (17.19). The Wanderer's memory of the ability is present but its manifestation is limited by the body (66.6). The adept's workings follow cycles counted from birth influences (64.10-64.11). The channel's telepathic ability is natural and cyclical (89.4). Genetic changes to the senses and the mind (18.19). When the channel asked whether she coasts on gifts from birth, Ra said "partially correct" without explaining (94.8, one entity, not a rule).
- **C27. Opened by work.** Wanderers get past the forgetting only through disciplined meditation and work (63.17). The veil is penetrated through green-ray activity, a compassionate love asking for nothing, which crystallizes higher centers until an adept emerges (83.18), with polarized partners and like-minded seekers helping, since "those of like mind which together seek shall far more surely find" (83.16-83.17). Energy centers are cleared in order from red upward, indigo is the adept's ray, and the discipline is know yourself, accept yourself, become the Creator (74.6, 74.8, 74.11). Dream recall is trained (86.10). The healing gateway is opened carefully and consciously (6.1), under specific exercises of mind, body and spirit (4.19).
- **C28. Both, by ability.** Ra describes two kinds of healer: those with an inclination, and those with no conscious orientation who have still opened the channel (4.14). Whether a visible gift appears is not a reliable sign of progress (58.23). Use of an ability in service is free will (65.6). GUIDE synthesis: Ra's pattern is that some abilities arrive with the entity (healing, Wanderers' talents, remembered skills), some arrive with a body or lineage (senses, cycles) and some are opened by practice (penetrating the veil, dream recall). Which sits where depends on the ability, not on one rule.

## Q4. Does a mix of kinds make abilities vary from entity to entity?

- **C29. The stated effect of the mix is on orientation and on Ra's service.** Earth's population comes from many places, which is unusual, and "The orientation, thus, has been difficult to unify" even with many teacher-learners (10.6). The questioner's framing, that the mixture of aware and unaware people limits Ra's contact, was called "basically correct", with the limit being "a maximal consideration of what is possible" (7.2, paraphrase). Not all of Earth's people share one race or origin (6.13). Each race has its own racial mind and adds to the planetary mind (91.7). That the mix makes abilities vary from entity to entity: **silent in the sessions checked.** What Ra does say about variation between individuals points elsewhere: earlier experience brought in (4.11, 65.12), free will in use (65.6), mind-complex activity (46.2) and work (83.18).

## For the design (a candidate for the owner, not a ruling; Ra 78.30-78.31 warns against forced one-to-one congruence, carried from lookup 1)

GUIDE synthesis from the above. Ra supports the idea that beings of different kinds start with different senses in these ways, and not in others:

1. **Stage-based starting sense.** A group-sense that is native at an early stage, veiled at the middle stage, and open again afterward (19.5-19.6, 90.12, 38.14). Ra ties this to the density a being comes from, not to a named star.
2. **A kind that starts with what others must earn.** The double-body entities show a fourth-density ability that Wanderers can reach only through disciplined work (63.16-63.17). This is Ra's clearest "one kind has it, another does not" case.
3. **Talents brought in per entity, limited in showing.** Offered before the start (65.12), remembered but hard to manifest (66.6). Which talent a character has could be a per-character choice before play, with its manifestation limited by the body.
4. **Opening by practice with a partner.** Ra's opening route is cooperative: love asking nothing, polarized partners, like-minded seekers (83.16-83.18). A sense like "read a neighbour's outcome" could open through repeated cooperation.
5. **A visible ability is a lesser signpost.** Ra says a gift is a weaker sign than what is sensed in the entity (58.23). That fits the owner's rule "fit, not worth": a kind's sense is a fit, not a rank.
6. **If kinds are mixed, the Ra-shaped effect is hard-to-unify orientation (10.6), not scrambled abilities.** Ra gives no table of senses per kind (silent in the sessions checked), so which kind gets which sense is the owner's choice, not Ra's.

No sense or ability in this file should be labelled "Ra's Sirius ability": none is given in the sessions checked.
