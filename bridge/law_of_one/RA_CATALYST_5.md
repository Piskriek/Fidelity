## Corrected answer

METHOD AND LIMITS. The guide fetched all 106 session pages from lawofone.info and keyword-searched all 2,523 Ra answers. That search missed Ra's word "calling" where it applies to negative entities. The verifier's full-text check found it (90.3, 90.4, 72.8, 24.8, 11.18, 21.20). This fresh reader re-read 90.3, 90.4, 72.7, 72.8, 24.8, 21.20, 11.18, 73.3, 87.9, 87.12, 87.17, 101.4 and 105.2 on lawofone.info with a fetch tool. That tool returns short verbatim fragments and uses a small model to pull them out, so the longer paraphrases below rely on the verifier's reading. Every statement that Ra does NOT say something comes from searches. None of them proves the text is absent. Transcript notes: the site's footnotes on 85.9, 85.10 and 85.11 say "service-to-others" should read "service-to-self", and 85.13 confirms this. 17.11 carries a density correction (fifth should be fourth). Quotes are Ra's words. Lines marked GUIDE synthesis are inference.

1. HELP ARRIVING AS SERVICE-TO-OTHERS INDIVIDUALS. Yes, in several forms.
- Wanderers. The Wanderers Ra describes in 12.26 belong to positive social memory complexes. They have the desire to serve and move toward the calling of "any entities who call for aid", from all reaches of creation (12.26). "Wanderer" alone does not mean service-to-others. Ra names rare negative Wanderers (36.16, 48.6, 36.17). Ra says all Wanderers come to assist (89.36), that they come when there is a call (89.39, 81.27), and that their missions run under a plan the Council of Nine approves (70.15). "The largest number" are sixth density (12.28).
- Forgetting. Ra gives two reasons: the genetic link between mind/body/spirit and cellular structure differs in third density, and the free will of third-density entities must be preserved (65.19). The forgetting can be penetrated far enough to remember identity and purpose, but not far enough to activate the denser bodies (65.19). The main danger is that the Wanderer "will forget its mission", become karmically involved and be swept into the maelstrom (12.28). Dysfunction can block their pre-incarnative intention (65.3), and they are as open to Orion influence as anyone (16.59).
- What Wanderers add. Once the forgetting is penetrated they add planetary love and light, serve "as beacon or shepherd", and offer a unique talent (65.12). Their help is not aimed at particular situations ahead of time: "Light and love go where they are sought and needed" (65.11).
- Named examples. Confederation sources gave information to Tesla, a Wanderer (11.25, 26.20). Jesus is a Wanderer who "received permission to perform this mission" (17.11, with the density correction). With Lincoln, the Earth entity chose to leave its vehicle and let another use it (26.15). A fourth-density being contacted it in sleep and "the exchange was made" (35.8). Ra states that this being was positive and service-to-others (26.17, 26.18, 35.8).
- Confederation members and teachers. They "wait for the calling", unlike the Orion group (8.12). They stay aware of a possible call for help while preserving the conditions for learning (21.14). They are "at the call of those upon your planet" (60.27). When a call is sincere but low in awareness, they offer only what is useful to that caller (60.27). Teachers speak when the requester is at the proper vibratory level (60.24). "Those upon the service-to-others path wait to be called and can only send love" (73.3). Contact avoids infringing free will, and Wanderers are the only subjects of thought-projection close encounters with positive social memory complexes (53.7). Ra describes permission to appear in thought-form (7.12) and the use of thought-forms to awaken Wanderers (53.12-53.13).
- Guides and inner-plane help. Every entity has "its guides or angelic presences and, due to polarization, teachers and friends" (69.5). Each has several guides (54.3). Angelic guardians include the higher self and inner-plane entities drawn by inner seeking (51.1). When sound vibrations are made with will and concentration, inner-plane beings respond as if they "received a telephone call" (74.12).
- Latwii, two separate events. In one, Latwii "was with the instrument as requested by the instrument" (98.5). In the other, Latwii was being channelled and did not let go during an Orion approach (68.5). Ra credits harmony, mutual love and the "honest calling for aid from the forces of light" together with giving the group its shield (87.6).
- The higher self and the guardians of the self. The higher self "protects when possible and guides when asked", and it does not manipulate (70.11). It aids life programming (36.2), but it can program only lessons and certain predisposing limitations (36.7). Its guidance can be used in either polarity (36.12). "There is no entity without help": help comes through awareness of unity or through guardians of the self (50.5).
- Conditions. The call: the first call counts as infinity, "each additional caller doubling or granting power to all the preceding call" (10.13), and "there is no time/space in call" (14.24). Free will: "The Guardians guard the free will distortion" (16.1). The veil: see 65.19 above.
- Limit on what help is. "It is impossible to help another being directly." One can only make catalyst available, and the most important catalyst is the radiated realization of oneness (17.2).

2. SERVICE-TO-SELF ENTITIES AS CATALYST. Ra's usual words are temptation, opportunity and offered. Ra uses "catalyst" once in this setting, taking it from the questioner.
- General statements. "The fourth-density habit is that of offering temptations and of energizing pre-existing distortions" (87.9). "Each successful temptation, each successful harvestable entity is a strengthener of the power and polarity" of that social memory complex (87.12). The light "emanating from attempts to be of service to others ... is another type of calling in that it represents that which requires balance by temptation" (90.3). "The temptations are offered by those negative entities of what you would call your inner planes" (90.4). Fourth-density negative groups reach out to less polarized entities to aid negative polarization (48.6). A negative Wanderer offers others "the opportunity to hear the information" (36.17).
- The psychic greeting of the Ra group. The Orion fifth-density entity makes an "attempt to be of service in its own way" (67.2, 68.5). Ra gives the sequence: fourth-density minions tempted, the group declined, and the fifth-density entity, acting "only reluctantly", moved to end the instrument's life by magical means (67.7, 62.20, 62.23). A prolonged greeting "has been offered in hopes that this entity would wish to leave the incarnation" (105.2). An Orion entity arranged an opportunity (25.1), and Ra uses "opportunity" for the temptations (67.23). Ra frames the attack as an "intensive opportunity" that "enables the instrument to continuously choose to serve others" and lets each member support her (67.21).
- The bite (101.4). The questioner asked which avenue "produced the catalyst of the bite". Ra answers: "The nature of catalyst is such that there is only one source." It continues: "the self as Creator, especially the higher self, is the base from which catalyst stands to offer its service." Only "in the sense which we feel you intend" was the source "the fifth-density, negative friend". Long-standing allergies and a weakened immune system also played a part.
- What is and is not greeting. Ra says the instrument's continual weariness is not due to the greeting (81.2). Ra also says parts of her symptoms may come from it (81.2). Some greeting encouraged the dizziness, though its source is largely her own determination (100.3). Psychic attack was only partly the cause of the kidney crisis (63.2). Ra separates the two by symptom and does not deny the greeting played a part.
- The group's response. Ra says their service is "gratefully acknowledged while being rejected as not being useful in your journey" (67.11).
- Serving the Creator. "It is impossible not to serve the Creator" (18.13). That answer concerns Confederation groups, and its "various distortions" refers to specialised Confederation groups, not to service-to-self. Ra extends the point elsewhere: "All are the Creator" (67.11). "The Law of One blinks neither at the light or the darkness" (7.15). The negative path is "an entirely acceptable method of self-knowledge of and by the Creator" (85.9-85.11, read with the errata). Ra adds that this path rests on an omission, a self-imposed darkness that cannot endure (85.11). For the densities above third, both polarities share the purpose of welcoming less distorted love/light (87.6).
- Interaction. Ra says there is "a good bit of interaction between the two polarities" in the attempt to sway third density. Fourth and fifth density are fairly independent, and in sixth the paths must take each other in (78.25).
- GUIDE synthesis. Ra's text supports that service-to-self temptations and greetings work as catalyst, or opportunity, for positively oriented entities (87.9, 90.3, 90.4, 67.21, 105.2, 101.4). There are three limits. First, Ra's own words are temptation, opportunity and offered, and "catalyst" appears when Ra accepts the questioner's phrase (101.4). Second, Ra makes the self as Creator, especially the higher self, the base of all catalyst (101.4). Third, where Ra states the entity's own aim, it is conquest, its own strengthening, or the instrument leaving the incarnation, not the recipient's growth (8.12, 72.8, 87.12, 67.7, 105.2). Ra calls it service "in its own way" (67.2) and nowhere describes it as an assigned role.

3. ASKED, CALLED, CHOSEN OR PERMITTED.
- Ra's word is "calling", and it applies to negative entities.
  - 90.3: "When a distortion which may be negatively connotated is effected, this calling occurs." The light of service-to-others is "another type of calling in that it represents that which requires balance by temptation." Third, there are avenues "made available by your fifth-density friend".
  - 90.4: the entities, "having come through the quarantine web", are "free to answer such calling." The temptations come from negative inner-plane entities, the "dark angels".
  - 72.8: negative entities can use the time/space windows only insofar as a calling balances the positive calling. Once here, their aim is conquest.
  - 24.8: when Orion came in about 3,600 years ago, "there was a proper calling for this information." "The ones of Orion have the working only according to their call."
  - 11.18: "Contact is made with those who call."
  - 21.20: "What is necessary to balance is opportunity." The balancing comes from the orientations "of those offering aid" and also "of those requesting aid."
  - 55.4: "a suppliant neophyte asking for aid in negative understanding" calls on Orion. This person asks for negative aid for themself, not for catalyst for others.
  - 16.2: the quarantine does not stop Orion's free will. It gives Orion a challenge.
  - 73.3: invoking negative entities also alerts the positive equivalent.
- The Orion group "calls itself to conquest" (8.12). This states its purpose. Read with 72.8 and 24.8, its access is still bounded by a calling.
- The doors a recipient opens. The Orion entity is "bound by the first distortion" but may "take advantage of any free will distortion". Its most promising target was the instrument's pre-incarnative physical distortions (68.16). Free will applies here only to the instrument, and Orion hopes to infringe without losing polarity (69.9). It "seeks out the distortions pre-incarnatively programmed and developed" (72.17). Pre-incarnative body distortions are energized "by those of negative polarity" (87.17). Leaving the body without protection makes an entity vulnerable (69.3). Intention draws its polar opposite in proportion to its intensity (72.7). The entity greets as close to the working as it can. When the instrument decided not to meditate, the greeting came at that point (98.3, 72.5). Ra describes this as timing and does not call it a needed opening. The entity passed the quarantine through "a very slight window" (67.19). Because positive groups are accepted, "the same opportunity must be offered to similarly wise negatively oriented entities" (67.20).
- Programming of catalyst. Catalyst is programmed for each entity's unique requirements (54.24, 46.16). Programs come from pre-incarnative choice or from reprogramming during incarnation (83.27, 33.6-33.7). Limits are set before incarnation (60.4). Less aware entities get catalyst that seems random until a bias forms (61.7, 92.11). Pre-incarnative choice can arrange protection, though Ra's example is narrow (95.25). In 50.5, pre-incarnative agreements with parents, siblings and others provide catalyst. That is a positive-path example, and no negative entity is named.
- Commissioned or agreed: none found. Neither the guide nor the verifier found Ra text in which the higher self, the Logos, the Confederation or a pre-incarnative agreement commissions a service-to-self entity to be catalyst. The only service-to-self agreement found is between two negative Wanderers working together (89.35). When a Wanderer blamed Orion for its anger, Ra pointed to "what pre-incarnative decisions it undertook to make", and neither confirmed nor denied Orion's part (59.3).
- The recipient did not ask. The Ra group declined the temptations (67.7) and rejected the service (67.11).
- GUIDE synthesis. The owner's "asked" fits Ra's "calling" in part. In Ra's account, negative entities are called and are free to answer (90.3, 90.4). Their access runs only as far as a calling balances the positive calling (72.8, 24.8, 21.20). The light of service-to-others is itself one such calling, because it "requires balance by temptation" (90.3). Three things differ from "asked":
  - Who calls is a distortion, the positive light, an avenue, or a negative seeker asking for their own negative aid. It is not a person or a planner asking them to be catalyst for someone.
  - Answering is their free choice.
  - They answer for their own aims (8.12, 72.8, 87.12, 69.9).
  Ra names no commission or agreement.

4. HOW RA DESCRIBES THE POSITIVE RESPONSE. These are descriptions of what the group did, not rules.
- Love and light toward the entity. Ra believes, while admitting limited understanding, that the group's love and light is "the most helpful catalyst which the group may offer to this entity" (67.8). Here the group is catalyst for the negative entity, the reverse direction. Ra describes the group as wishing unaligned entities love, light, peace and joy and bidding them well (67.11). Ra says offering the instrument's life is unlikely to be found a true service (67.11).
- Without fear. The instrument adapted "without undue distortions towards fear", so the attack did not succeed, though it drained her (67.2). An early answer gives protection as thanksgiving, seeing self and other-self as Creator, an open heart and praising the light: "This is all the protection necessary" (32.1). Later answers add ritual protection, for example light forms that build a wall (72.8, 72.17).
- Acceptance, not control. Catalyst may be loved and accepted or controlled (46.16). "Acceptance is the key to positively polarized use of catalyst", and control is the negative key (46.9, in an example about anger). The balanced entity sees the causes behind a seeming attack and is "open to many more opportunities for service" (42.3). For a seeker who has purely chosen service-to-others, "all that is encountered speaks of the love and the light", though there is no outward shelter from cruel catalyst (95.23, 95.24). On the positive path the entity increasingly chooses positive interpretations of catalyst, and the reverse holds for service-to-self (94.12).
- Effect on polarity. After declining, the group showed "no significant deviations from the desire for a purely other-self service orientation" (67.7). It twice refused chances to separate self from other-self (105.2). For a different entity (Martin), Ra hedges that he "may be seen to have polarized more towards the positive" (34.10). A negative entity is strengthened by each successful temptation (87.12) and depolarized when it fails to control (87.6). At social-memory-complex scale, the Confederation's defensive stance in the thought-war loses "some polarity ... due to this friction", and both sides are depleted (25.5, 25.6).

## For the design: candidates only, the owner rules

These are my mapping, not Ra's words and not rulings.

- **Helpers as individuals (A follows).** A service-to-others helper character (a Wanderer) could be placed into a world in answer to a call, with its memory veiled. What it gives is catalyst offered, not rescue (17.2). Its being a Wanderer does not by itself make it service-to-others: Ra names rare negative Wanderers.
- **Service-to-self beings as catalyst (B-1a follows; B-2a partly; B-2b silent):**
  - such a character offers temptation or opportunity, by its own free choice and for its own aims;
  - it reaches a recipient only as far as that recipient's own calling or distortions open a door (72.8, 24.8, 68.16);
  - the recipient may decline (67.11);
  - the polarizing choice is the recipient's response: acceptance rather than control (46.16), love and light sent to the offerer (67.8).

  Nothing in Ra says such beings are commissioned or agreed with, so the game should not assign them as catalyst. The base of all catalyst is the self, especially the higher self (101.4). That matches the owner's ruling that pain is chosen: the door is the recipient's `chose=`, and intensity only goes down.
