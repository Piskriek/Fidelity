# Ra lookup 9: distortion, pain, fear, and what pulls and shapes an entity (2026-11-02)

**Asked because** the owner (Piskriek, they/them) said, as one viewpoint that is theirs and is not judged: "excitement pulls and logic pushes the entities, pain is the result of distorted logic and fear is the result of distorted excitement". The aim is to give a small simulated world (a being guesses what the world will answer for each choice and picks by how much it values the guesses) Ra's labels for each part.

**Scope.** Ra sessions 1-106 on lawofone.info only. Builds on lookups 1, 2, 5 and 7 and does not repeat them. Those lookups already settle: distortion is not pejorative and only the Law of One is not a distortion (lookup 1); catalyst is a learning mechanism, accepted or controlled (lookup 5); the entity programs its own catalyst and some catalyst is random (lookup 7).

## Q1. What does Ra mean by "distortion"?

- **D1** (RA, paraphrase) Ra separates undistorted unity from what appears once unity focuses. The rhythms of intelligent infinity are without distortion, so distortion begins with differentiation. (27.7)
- **D2** (RA, paraphrase + fragment) Three primal distortions: free will, then Logos (the Creative Principle, Love), then Light ("a distortion known as Light"). After the first few, the many distortions are "equal one to another". Ra confirms Love as the second distortion and calls it the great activator and primal co-Creator. (15.21, 27.11, 27.12, 27.13)
- **D3** (RA, paraphrase) The Law of One has no multiplicity and so is not a distortion. Other so-called laws are distortions of it, and some distortions are primal and important for progress. (22.27)
- **D4** (RA, paraphrase + fragment) Ra calls "the term, distortion" the key to a question about suggestion. Any distortion, physical, mental or spiritual, can be accentuated by someone able to change consciousness. The less it is balanced by self-knowledge, the easier it is to exploit. Ra gives no verdict on the condition itself. (80.4)
- **D5** (RA, fragments) Ra names pain itself a distortion: "the distortion known as pain", and says the questioner's attitude yields "helpful distortions" for evolution (83.25). Also "the distortion called pain" (60.8) and "the distortion towards pain" (60.31). Paraphrase: parts of the body sensitised by great distortions toward pain (102.14).
- **D6** (RA, fragment) Ra says the weakening of the physical body was "designed to distort entities towards a predisposition to deal with each other". A deliberate distortion, used as a method. (19.13)
- **D7** (RA, paraphrase) Distortions are balanced by opposite distortions until balance is reached, not removed (22.26). Ra names a service-to-self "distortion of the manipulation of others" (22.5), and calls the shortened life span a distortion of the Law of One that acts as a protective adjustment (22.6).
- **D8** (RA, fragment) "a distortion which may be negatively connotated". The qualifier implies a distortion is not negative by default. (90.3)
- **D9** (RA, paraphrase) Ra declines the verb "allow" for how the first distortion relates to blockage, because "allow" implies a right-versus-wrong polarity. The Law of Confusion gives each complex free reach for its energies. (54.13)
- **D10** (RA, fragment) "the source is the limit of the viewpoint", said as the general rule for distortions, before three suggestions for a group member's anger, frustration and dull pain at the indigo centre. (99.5, confirmed on a second fetch)
- **D11** (RA, paraphrase + fragment) Distortion also names what a blockage produces. A blocked yellow-ray centre often shows "distortions towards power, manipulation" (15.12). Planetary and personal blockages distort intelligent energy (15.9).
- **D12** (GUIDE synthesis) Ra's bare word is the neutral baseline: it covers free will, Love, Light, pain and the body's design (D1, D2, D5, D6), and Ra adds qualifiers when it means "gone wrong". Those qualifiers are blocked (D11, 87.21), unbalanced (D4, 60.31), negatively connotated (D8), and, for catalyst, "gone awry" (34.6) or "fails in its design" (46.16). In Ra's sense "excitement" and "logic" would already be distortions of unity in their healthy form. The owner's "distorted logic" maps closer to Ra's "unbalanced" or "blocked" than to the bare word.

## Q2. Pain: distorted thinking, blockage, or unintegrated catalyst?

- **P1** (RA, paraphrase + fragment) Pain is the most common catalyst, mostly mental/emotional, sometimes physical, rarely spiritual. It offers patience, tolerance and the light touch. When grief turns to bitterness, Ra calls it "catalyst which has gone awry" and says more catalyst will be provided. (34.6)
- **P2** (RA, paraphrase) Before the veil, pain was a warning signal to move the body, and the mind could then cut it off. The veil made pain harder to control, creating a catalyst not usable before. Ra does not simply agree that cutting off pain removes catalyst. The attitude toward pain varies. (83.20, 83.23, 83.24, 83.25, 83.26)
- **P3** (RA, paraphrase + fragment) Some entities have an "esurient" catalyst program, chosen before incarnation or reprogrammed in life. It "may indeed seem a great waste". Ra suggests hoping the other-self grasps what it offered itself. (83.27)
- **P4** (RA, paraphrase) Catalyst is unconscious and does not work with intelligence (46.14). Many cases of catalyst are not used (46.15). All catalyst is designed to offer experience, which may be loved and accepted or controlled. When neither is chosen, the catalyst "fails in its design" and the entity goes on until catalyst strikes it, forming a bias toward acceptance and love or toward separation and control. (46.16) This is Ra's nearest wording for unintegrated catalyst.
- **P5** (RA, paraphrase) A positively oriented entity may program a narrow path and conditions that give physical pain if it strays (54.21). A negative entity usually programs wealth and ease, but may choose a painful condition to hone anger or hatred (54.22).
- **P6** (RA, paraphrase) For the instrument's own pain Ra gives physical and pre-incarnative causes: arthritis and lupus from a pre-incarnative choice, with affirmations that may help (84.3); earlier surgery, lupus and nerve damage (94.3); overactivity in weak parts of the body (58.2). None names reasoning.
- **P7** (RA, paraphrase + fragment) For the instrument, Ra says it chose a life needing self-acceptance without others' acceptance, to balance wisdom with compassion. Two balancing workings, accepting its limits and accepting others' love, will aid "release from the distortion called pain". (60.8)
- **P8** (RA, paraphrase) An entity out of harmony with its circumstances first feels inner heat, and prolonged disharmony may appear as blocked or uncontrolled growth, such as cancer (60.20). A weak pendulum swing means a partial blockage; a strong one, overstimulation. Both are unbalanced (60.31).
- **P9** (RA, paraphrase) For one illness, Ra says a yellow-ray blockage was the opening through which it entered (102.2). The remedy is loving all in relationship and hoping only for their joy, peace and comfort (102.11). Instrument-specific. The fetch did not find pain tied to a blockage in general.
- **P10** (RA, paraphrase) Uncontrolled anger can drive cells out of control (40.9). Illnesses can serve as self-revelation and respond to self-healing once the cause is understood (40.12). A positive entity accepts and loves anger; a negative one controls it. A negative entity that cannot control its anger may grow a cancer-like growth. (46.9, 46.10, 46.11)
- **P11** (RA, paraphrase) With experience, an entity increasingly reads catalyst positively on the service-to-others path and negatively on the service-to-self path (94.12). Ra confirms that balance should hold even through extreme pain or loss of life, and calls this principal to balance (42.5).
- **P12** (RA, paraphrase + fragment) An entity dwelling completely in unity is harmed only by the death of the physical vehicle, and other pain is "as nothing" (95.25). Protection from random catalyst comes from a pre-incarnative choice or complete dwelling in unity. An entity whose Experience of the Mind is negative has no such protection (95.26).
- **P13** (RA, paraphrase) In fourth density, physical pain plays a minimal role, mostly near the end of an incarnation (weariness). Mental and spiritual pain are the main catalysts. (43.9, 43.10)
- **P14** (RA, paraphrase) Before the veil, pain was not frightening, and there was no effort to serve for love or benefit from fear. (82.22)
- **P15** (RA, paraphrase) A senior entity may try to learn so much in one life that the intensity of catalyst disarranges it, and the experience is "not maximally useful" (48.8). The shortened life span is a protective adjustment so an entity does not receive more than it can bear (22.6).
- **P16** (RA, fragment + paraphrase) In one case Ra links great pain and a loss of outer service to the entity's free-will decision not to address the physical catalyst with a prescribed drug (103.4, wording approximate). Refusing drugs to "dull the pain that sharpens the catalyst" is called a first occasion (103.5).
- **P17** (RA, paraphrase) The planet's end-of-cycle catalyst is like a body's pain and disease acting on it, which Ra confirms (65.16). Physical pain catalyst belongs to the unmanifested self (33.16).
- **P18** (GUIDE synthesis) The passages read give Ra's origins of pain as: a designed catalyst (P1, P4); a warning function (P2); programmed before birth (P5, P6); physical conditions (P6); disharmony or uncontrolled anger (P8, P10); random (P12, and lookup 7). Reasoning named as a cause: silent in the sessions checked, supported by the passages above, a complete search for "logic" (4 hits, none a Ra statement of this kind) and for "rational" (5 hits). Ra puts the mind's role in the response: acceptance or control (P4), souring (P1), attitude (P2), reading catalyst by polarity (P11), self-acceptance as a release (P7).

## Q3. Fear: a distortion of desire, will, expectation or love? Fear in the energy centres? Fear as catalyst?

- **F1** (RA, fragment + paraphrase) The energy transfer is "blocked only if one or both entities have fear of possession", or of being possessed, or desire for possession or being possessed. The transcript's third-ray label carries a footnote correcting it to the fourth (green) ray. (26.38)
- **F2** (RA, paraphrase) Ra lists four distortions: fear of possession, desire for possession, fear of being possessed, desire to be possessed. They share one source, the possession ray. Green-ray activation is always vulnerable to the yellow ray, mostly, and sometimes orange. These distortions deactivate green-ray transfer (32.14). Yellow is relation to groups, orange is power over individuals, and either as the primary pattern leads to blockage and "insatiable hunger" (32.2).
- **F3** (RA, paraphrase + fragment) Ra gives the root cause of blockage as the inability to see the other-self as the Creator, and puts it another way as "the lack of love". (87.21)
- **F4** (RA, paraphrase) Ray by ray, the fetched answers name these motives, none of them fear. Orange: desire for power over others (31.14), a sexual blockage that came from people's free choice, with Orion intensifying (31.15), and heightened hunger from the first blockage after the veil (84.18). Yellow: distortions toward power and manipulation (15.12). Red: blockage linked to overcrowding that dulls the desire to serve (31.9). The south pole, which Ra links to the root centre, is where experience comes in, and the root's first response is basically survival; once balanced, the higher centres can use the experience (50.2). Fear in the red, orange or yellow ray blockages by name: silent in the sessions checked, apart from F2.
- **F5** (RA, paraphrase) Fear is set against will and love. Had fear grown greater than the will to persevere when breath was short, each breath would have been harder (96.2). Love met the fifth-density entity's fear weapon and reduced it (80.3). The instrument's strong will and lack of hysteria let the attack fail (67.2).
- **F6** (RA, paraphrase) Thought-form entities may take any form tied to fear or terror (16.45) and feed on fear (43.6). The fears of the mass consciousness shape which body parts are removed (43.5).
- **F7** (RA, paraphrase) Fear and doom in a contact point to a negative source, while hope and a stronger desire to serve point to the Confederation (53.17). Confederation awakening of Wanderers avoids causing fear (53.12). UFO flaps cause many fears (48.5). Fifth-density negative Wanderers are few because they fear the forgetting (48.6).
- **F8** (RA, paraphrase) After the Maldek war, survivors were left in "a social complex knot or tangle of fear". Confederation later untied it (10.1).
- **F9** (RA, paraphrase) Ra called the questioner "basically correct" about a mild fear response toward an animal and a more emotional one toward another self. The "fear response" wording is the questioner's. Ra adds that a balanced entity sees more complex causes behind another self's attack and gains more opportunities for service (42.3).
- **F10** (GUIDE synthesis) Fear is not a named catalyst in the passages read. It appears as a response to catalyst, and as a lever (F5, F6, F7). Ra puts fear and desire side by side as twin possession distortions (F1, F2), and Ra's root of blockage is lack of love (F3). Whether fear follows from desire: silent in the sessions checked.

## Q4. Pull and push: what draws an entity forward, what shapes or constrains it?

**What draws (Ra's words: will, desire, seeking, attraction)**

- **U1** (RA, fragment) For why some learn faster, Ra points to "the function of the will" and "the attraction to the upward spiraling line of light". (28.14)
- **U2** (RA, paraphrase) Incoming energy is pulled upward according to the distortions of each energy centre and the strength of will or desire from awareness of the inner light. Ra asked for a more specific query. (54.29) The closest Ra pairing of a pull and a shaping filter.
- **U3** (RA, paraphrase) The inner light's strength matches the will to seek it. Seeking draws upward-spiraling light (57.14). Intense seeking attracts the light energy of all things, and the realisation of the One occurs where inner seeking meets it (57.33).
- **U4** (RA, paraphrase) Upward-spiraling light is "drawn by the will" to meet the inner light. (73.8)
- **U5** (RA, paraphrase) Intention or desire attracts its polar opposite as intensely as the intention is strong. Will and desire for service activate the matching polarity. (72.7)
- **U6** (RA, paraphrase + fragment) Ra names "the faculty of will or pure desire", as perhaps the most important function to arise from the veiling. It is not a mental function in itself; it arises from the potential the veiling created. (85.19)
- **U7** (RA, paraphrase) Each density is pulled forward in evolution by the attraction of the following ray (40.3). First density is attracted toward growth through the attraction of upward-spiraling light, and Ra says no part of evolution is random (41.10). Fourth-density experience can begin with an added attraction for an entity serving others in a troubled third-density setting (63.14).
- **U8** (RA, paraphrase) The south (negative) pole, which Ra links to the red-ray root centre, pulls unto itself whatever is magnetised to it (50.2). Experience is drawn to the entity because that is how catalyst enters awareness (50.3). So the entity also pulls catalyst toward itself.
- **U9** (RA, paraphrase) Pure negativity acts as a gravity well pulling all into it (70.23). In a nearly pure negative social complex the pull on other-selves is constant and the free will is bent on conquest (71.14).
- **U10** (RA, paraphrase) The indigo (form-making) body draws on the will and uses its wisdom to choose the place and kind of experience. (51.10)
- **U16** (RA, paraphrase) The Creator wants to know itself (27.8). Third density is a choice and a short period for that choice, the axis on which creation turns (76.15, 76.16).

**What shapes or constrains**

- **U11** (RA, fragment + paraphrase) The rational/intuitive mind is "at once the great virtue and the great handicap of third density" (19.13); it combines rational and intuitive thinking (19.11).
- **U12** (RA, paraphrase) Intuitive and rational lobes use weak electrical energy alike and serve either polarity. The analytical mind may have somewhat more to work with in polarizing, and unchecked intuition can hinder it. The two must be balanced. (49.4)
- **U13** (RA, paraphrase) The Experience of the Mind shapes how catalyst is interpreted, increasingly positive or negative by polarity. (94.12)
- **U14** (RA, fragment) Catalyst, when neither acceptance nor control is chosen, goes on "until catalyst strikes it", forming a bias. (46.16)
- **U15** (RA, paraphrase) Ra calls the matrix consciousness ("the Magician") and the potentiator the unconscious (78.11). Ra does not tie them to will, desire, reason or understanding in what was read. Ra says the archetypes and densities relate tangentially, with no congruency, and warns against insisting on strict one-to-one fit (78.30, 78.31). The archetypes are not used here.
- **U17** (GUIDE synthesis) Ra's two-part line (U2, U10) is a pull (will, desire) and a shaping filter (the distortions of each centre, or wisdom), not "reason". Ra gives push-like roles to catalyst (U14) and to the body's designed weakness (D6). The site search for "push" returned one result, unrelated. Ra's "Logos" (15.21) is Love, the Creative Principle, so the owner's "logic" does not map onto it.

**Own triangle**

- **T1** (GUIDE synthesis) Desire/will, the shaping mind, and the world's answer (catalyst) are each bridged by Ra citations: desire to mind (U2, U10); mind to catalyst (U13, U14); catalyst to desire (U8 experience is drawn; U5 intention draws its opposite). The closed triangle is observed in Ra's text, not forced onto it.
