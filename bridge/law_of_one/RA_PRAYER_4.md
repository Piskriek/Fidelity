## Corrected answer

SHORT VERDICT. Ra supports the multiplying half of B. A united calling is multiplied, and a lack of unity costs a slight loss (7.3-7.5, 10.13). Seekers of like mind who seek together find far more surely (83.17). Ra gives no rule that praying at the same time or in the same place is what counts (GUIDE synthesis, from absence). Ra splits the timing half of B. The cycle gates open like a clock whatever the circumstances (9.4, 13.23). But how many gates the harvest takes depends on readiness. If entities seek and become one within a major cycle, the planet is vacated at the end of that cycle instead of after three (20.27, 6.15). Ra ties no timing effect to prayer. On A, Ra's positive beings wait to be called (73.3), and Ra's own contacts followed a calling it judged sufficient (23.1, 23.6, 27.1). The one unasked activity Ra records is limited thought-form appearances and mystery. Its stated aim is that people grasp infinity, not that they pray (7.12). The verifier checked the cited passages. In this pass I re-fetched 2.2, 6.15, 7.12, 9.4, 10.13, 14.14-14.15, 20.27, 27.1, 40.8, 43.13, 64.4, 65.12, 72.7, 73.3-73.4 and 83.17. Refs marked [verifier] were not re-fetched.

1. THE CALL AND HOW IT MULTIPLIES
1a. The service the Confederation can offer equals the square of the need of the calling, divided by or integrated with the Law of One in its free-will distortion (7.1). The Law of Service squares the desires of those who call (7.2).
1b. The square is sequential, so ten callers do not make 100 (7.3). For ten callers Ra squares one ten sequential times (7.4) and gives about 1,012. That is slightly less than a full squaring, because callers are sometimes not totally unified, a statistical loss over the period of call (7.5).
1c. Ra restates the mechanism (10.13). The first call equals infinity and is not counted; it is the cornerstone. The second call is added. From the third call on, each caller doubles all the preceding call.
1d. GUIDE synthesis, uncertain: Ra does not show how 1,012 arises. One possible reading is 2^10 = 1,024 less the loss. But a literal reading of 10.13 gives fewer than ten doublings for ten callers. The ten comes from 7.4, and 1,012 could also be a digit count or exponent (7.7 speaks of many digits). Ra states none of these readings.
1e. About 352,000 call Ra and 632,000,000 call the Confederation. Ra says these numbers are simplified (7.6). The squared total is roughly meaningless in finite terms, but it is a great calling that demands service (7.7).
1f. The same structure appears elsewhere. A social memory complex's Oversoul works by squares, which Ra says people prefer to call doubling (37.7). Ra confirms the questioner's statement that Orion uses the Law of Squares or Doubling (62.15). Ra calls such laws distortions; there is only one law (22.27). Ra applies the cornerstone-and-doubling pattern to one person's repeated seeking (10.14 [verifier]). Ra does not affirm that absorbing members doubles a complex's power (87.13-87.14 [verifier]).
1g. Seeking together. Ra names the doubling effect: "Those of like mind which together seek shall far more surely find" (83.17). The context is polarized pairs piercing the veil, not harvest. Several wills acting as one, one-pointed in service, can alert a great mass of light strength, which works under free will and must be invoked (72.7). Ra credits this group's effectiveness partly to its meditation periods together (12.1 [verifier]). Ra calls group worship, including combined prayers of thanksgiving, a great protection. Its disciplined, purified emotion may be the key to the gateway to intelligent infinity (64.4).
1h. Same place or time. None of these passages makes calling in one place or at one moment count more. The variables Ra names are unity (7.5), like-mindedness (83.17) and will (72.7, 73.4, 74.13). GUIDE synthesis: Ra counts the 632,000,000 callers (7.6) without mentioning any gathering. Ra says there is no time/space in call (14.24), but there it is answering who will hear and understand, including late callers. Applying it to simultaneity is GUIDE synthesis.
1i. Prayer and will. Whether praying helps depends wholly on the intentions and objects of the one who prays (49.8). A calling's efficacy depends on the will, seeking, concentration and conscious knowledge of those who call (74.13). The context there is church incantation versus the adept's working. Positive adepts call on light in proportion to the strength and purity of their will to serve (73.4).

2. THE RESPONSE COMES AFTER THE CALL
2a. The harvest at the gate of about 50,000 years ago was none (14.15). Afterwards the Confederation only stayed aware of a possible call for help (21.14, 21.15). The first call, from Maldek entities about 46,000 years ago, was answered with love and light, as pure undifferentiated love rather than concepts (21.16, 21.17).
2b. Service-to-others entities wait to be called and can only send love (73.3). As harvest nears, the forces of light work according to their call. Orion's call is far smaller, the squares create resistance to penetration, and free will is kept (24.8). The Confederation is at the call of those on the planet. To a sincere call with little awareness it can offer only information useful to that caller (60.27; the guide cited 60.26). If called, Ra will serve with all its strength (65.15).
2c. Open landing is not decided by the number calling. It needs a consensus of a whole social memory complex, approved by the Council of Saturn. Among Earth's peoples this has been possible only in isolated instances (7.8).
2d. The Atlantean visitations needed a group whose square overcame the integrated resistance, plus Confederation naivete (22.25). The crystal power was later turned to non-healing ends (22.23). "Perverted" is the questioner's word in 22.27, which Ra accepts with one exception.
2e. A calling can be prepared. One entity partly prepared the consciousness of the Egyptian people, so that they could offer the calling that let Ra walk among them (27.1). Ra can contact only those already seeking the One (14.26 [verifier]).
2f. Those who call cannot always understand the answer (14.24).

3. INTERVENTION, WORSHIP, FREE WILL
3a. The Law of Confusion is Ra's name for what humans call free will, a primal distortion of the Law of One (3.14). It is utterly paramount (72.7).
3b. Ra did not want to be worshiped as builder of a miraculous pyramid, so the pyramid was made to look built rather than thought (3.14). The scope is that case. Ra does not state it as a general rule.
3c. Egypt. About 18,000 years ago Ra found no appropriate calling and left without acting (23.1). Later it judged the calling sufficient, laid its plan before the Council and walked among the people (23.6). Its craft were visible, which made no difference because the people already took wondrous events as normal (23.2, 23.3, 23.5). The confusion came from Ra's embodied presence: thirty impressions per word, so Ra withdrew after a short period (23.6). Ra walked among Earth's peoples only at that time (14.5). After Akhenaten its teachings were quickly perverted (23.6).
3d. Ra reached the pharaoh Akhenaten by communication, not by walking among people. He decreed the Law of One, his priests gave lip service only, and belief in many gods returned (2.2; Ra's earlier departure is in 1.5 [verifier]). Ra sees itself as a naive Confederation member still working to repair the damage (22.26 [verifier]). In South America the teachings were grossly perverted into human sacrifice (23.16 [verifier]). GUIDE synthesis: in every case Ra records where a visit produced a religion or teaching, it decayed.
3e. Limited unasked activity. The Council of Saturn has not allowed the quarantine to be broken. The Confederation may appear in thought-form to those who have eyes to see, and may minister so as to cause mystery, so that people become aware of infinite possibility. Ra says the gateway to the Law of One opens only when people grasp infinity (7.12). No call is mentioned in that answer. Ra also felt a strong need to involve its thought-forms to balance perverted information (26.20). Worship is not mentioned there.
3f. Fourth density chooses not to be visible to third. Ra's stated reason is the need to concentrate on the difficult third-density vibration, not free will (12.17). In meetings between positive social memory complexes and Wanderers, infringing free will is greatly undesired, so Wanderers are the only subjects of the thought projections that make up close encounters (53.7).
3g. Orion. A mass Orion landing would strongly increase service-to-self polarization (16.8). Yet Orion prefers to work through recruits, because a mass landing infringes free will and costs polarization. Ra calls a landing a gamble: if the planet were conquered, free will would be re-established. Orion's desire to progress keeps it from breaking the Law of Confusion (16.9). Negative entities impressed the Ten Commandments, aping positivity while keeping negative traits (16.15; Orion is named in 16.16). Orion can use windows only insofar as some calling balances the positive calling (72.8 [verifier]; 24.8).

4. HARVEST TIMING
4a. Gates on a clock. The cycles move as precisely as a clock strikes the hour. The gateway to intelligent infinity opens on the hour whatever the circumstances, and each planet is on its own schedule (9.4). Fourth density approaches as regularly as a clock striking the hour (13.23). There are three major cycles of about 25,000 years, with harvest possible at each. After three, all are harvested regardless of progress (6.15).
4b. How many gates. If entities can seek and become one in a moment, they may go forward in a moment. Were this to happen in a major cycle, the third-density planet would be vacated at the end of that cycle (20.27). The typical case is a small harvest after the first cycle, a larger one after the second, and completion at the third (20.27). So readiness can complete a planet's harvest at an earlier gate, while the gate times themselves stay fixed (20.27, 9.4). The guide read 20.27 as a fixed date regardless of unity. That reading is wrong.
4c. Earth now. Ra says the harvest is now (14.14). The harvest at the gate of about 50,000 years ago was none, within the questioner's 75,000-year frame, which Ra does not correct (14.15). GUIDE synthesis: on that frame, Earth's present harvest is the last gate of 6.15, so no earlier gate remains for Earth. Ra does not say this in those words.
4d. Individuals. One who penetrates intelligent infinity can be harvested at any time in the cycle, and few have (11.8; also 34.2 [verifier]).
4e. Fourth density. Its cycle is about 30 million years unless entities can be harvested sooner. There the harvest is completely a function of the social memory complex's readiness (43.13).
4f. Pace of transition. The third-to-fourth transition is estimated at 100 to 700 years, which Ra says cannot be accurate because of the peoples' volatility. In the same answer Ra says the third-density climate of planetary consciousness is slowing the process (40.8). The realignment will come with some inconvenience because of the peoples' thought-forms, and the harvest will still be small (17.1). GUIDE synthesis: Ra states only the slowing direction. It never says better consciousness would speed things up.
4g. Dates are hedged. 2011 is the questioner's date; Ra never says 2011 [verifier]. Ra calls it an approximation and an appropriate probable/possible nexus (17.29).
4h. Asked about coming planetary changes, Ra says it does not concern itself with the conditions that bring harvest (1.9). Earth changes are a symptom of a difficult harvest (33.12). Ra declines to count numbers (65.15).
4i. The verifier searched all 106 sessions. It found no passage tying group meditation or prayer to harvest timing, and none tying harvest to sooner, earlier or hasten. This absence is GUIDE synthesis.

5. WHAT SEEKING CHANGES: NUMBER AND VIBRATION
5a. Wanderers, teachers and adepts are bent on increasing the harvest, yet there are few to harvest (13.23). Those who want to consciously raise the planetary vibration will find visualization particularly satisfying (49.8).

5d. No one can help another directly; one can only offer catalyst, chiefly by radiating one's own realization of oneness (17.2). Ra's "no shortcuts" line there concerns an individual's enlightenment, not the harvest date (17.2).

6. THE GROUP AS SOCIAL MEMORY COMPLEX
6a. A social complex becomes a social memory complex when the whole group is of one orientation or seeking, and its lost memory becomes known to the group (11.17). The purpose of a social memory complex is evolution, and fourth and sixth densities need it (45.11). It is properly a fourth-density phenomenon. Positive third-density ones are quite rare, and no negative ones are known (38.7). The Confederation formed by voluntary sharing, and each member keeps its own service (16.32).

6d. GUIDE synthesis, limited to third density: Ra does not describe a social memory complex as moving the third-density gate. In fourth density, the complex's readiness does set harvest timing (43.13).
