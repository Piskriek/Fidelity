# Ra lookup 7: perception, the higher self and catalyst, teaching and safety (2026-11-02)

**Asked because** the owner said (one viewpoint, theirs): "when the game engine is a fractal as well the entities who play the higherselves have the ability to ask the parts of the game to change, the engine acts as a teacher keeping them safe". The owner also said telepathy is a starting skill in the Sirius system, and that on Earth it depends on the kind of entity.

**Method.** A Sonnet guide read lawofone.info. A Haiku verifier then fetched each cited session and checked it. Both worked from WebFetch summaries, so wording is approximate. Raw output: `RA_PERCEPTION_TEACHER_7_raw.json`.

**Verifier tally:** 40 claims checked, 37 supported and 3 partly. All four labels were judged fair, with caveats attached.

## What the sessions checked say (paraphrased)

**Perception and the veil**
- Second density counts the pack as self, and communication with second-density beings was telepathic, by their nature (19.15, 19.5).
- Third density is self-aware and veiled. The veil sits between the conscious and unconscious mind and filters catalyst. Ra says it is not a limit on the physical senses (86.6–86.7, 94.10–94.12).
- Ra offers as a supposition, not a fact, that speech and the opposable thumb intensified the veil, favouring speech over telepathy (90.12).
- In fourth density, thoughts and feelings are open to view, so there is little deception. Fourth-density beings choose not to be visible to third density (50.7, 13.20).
- No veils above third density (77.14). Inner planes are real (17.36–17.39).
- A list of senses per density: silent in the sessions checked.

**The higher self and catalyst**
- The higher self is the self in mid-sixth density, the future self (70.8–70.10). Late sixth density also appears (36.12, 37.6); the sessions checked do not reconcile the two.
- Before incarnation it helps program the lessons and certain limitations. The rest is the entity's free choice (36.2, 36.7). An entity conscious of the process programs its own catalyst (54.21–54.22).
- During a life, the higher self does not manipulate. It protects when possible, guides when asked, and waits at the door of the veil (70.11, 83.3). The ask runs from the incarnate self to the higher self (60.4, 70.11, 75.36).
- Not all catalyst is chosen: planetary catastrophes are random (33.12). The verifier marked this summary "partly": Ra names the entity, not the higher self, as the programmer of its catalyst.

**Teaching and safety**
- Teaching and learning run both ways. A teacher cannot learn for the student; only catalyst can be offered.
- The Law of Confusion (free will) limits help. Help is called for, not imposed, and large interventions in the environment have gone badly.
- Catalyst is part of a mechanism of learning and teaching set up by the sub-Logos, but Ra says that mechanism does not work with intelligence (46.14). All catalyst is designed to offer experience (46.16).
- Protection exists — guardians, guides, the higher self — against permanent separation and infringement. It does not remove catalyst or harm, and catalyst can be painful or intense (50.5, 51.1, 83.27, 48.8).

## Notes for the design (candidates for the owner, not rulings)

- **Telepathy and density.** In Ra's account, telepathy belongs to second density's group-self, the veil hides it in third density (Ra's supposition ties that to speech), and fourth density makes thought transparent. A world in which a witness-like sense opens up after harvest would echo that. So would the owner's "the group grows with density" (lookup 6: unity deepens with density). The probes found that seeing a neighbour's outcome carries information in Four Valleys (24% less uncertainty), because about half the kinds each season are shared.
- **Who asks.** Ra's direction is: the incarnate being asks, and the higher self or the guides answer, within free will. A teacher-engine where the being (or its player) asks and the engine's parts answer would follow Ra more closely than the higher self asking the engine mid-life.
- **The owner's pain ruling stands.** Felt pain is a higher-self choice, never a setting, and intensity only goes down. Ra's own account (the entity programs its catalyst; some catalyst is random) is noted here as a different viewpoint, not as a correction of the ruling.
