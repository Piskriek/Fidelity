## Corrected answer

1. Ra times harvest by cycle. Major cycles are about 25,000 years, and three make a 75,000-76,000-year master cycle. Harvest is possible at the end of each major cycle, and at the end of the master cycle "all are harvested regardless of their progress". The gateway opens, and fourth density approaches, as regularly as "a clock upon the hour" (6.15, 9.4, 13.23).
2. For Earth, asked about the questioner's year 2011, Ra said "This is an approximation" and "an appropriate probable/possible time/space nexus for harvest". In 1981 it said "The harvest is now", though in session 6 it had said "The harvesting is not yet" (17.29, 14.14, 6.19).
3. Ra names three levels "watching over harvest" (51.1):
   - the mind/body/spirit complex totality or higher self, with inner-plane entities drawn by the entity's seeking;
   - Confederation members "at the edge of the steps of light/love", who catch those who stumble;
   - Guardians "from the octave above our own", who provide "the precise emissions of light/love" by which each vibration is ascertained.
4. The harvest itself is "automatic": each entity responds by its violet-ray emanation, "unchangeable during harvest" (51.1, 48.7, 34.16). Ra never says the Logos performs it. Ra says a Logos' biasing "cannot change the requirements for achieving harvestability", without saying who set them (90.23; 82.21; 93.5) [GUIDE reading].
5. For positive harvest Ra confirms the questioner's more-than-50% service-to-others figure and itself uses 51%. For negative harvest it gives about 5% to others and 95% to self, "approach[ing] totality", with a "sinkhole of indifference" between. It values balance over brilliance, and each harvested entity enters the fourth sub-density matching its understanding (17.31-17.34, 41.19).
6. Ra confirms the questioner's three-way split with one correction:
   - the positively harvested "remain in this planetary influence but not upon this plane";
   - the negatively harvestable go elsewhere (Ra's probability vortex indicated a small negative harvest for Earth);
   - "the great majority" repeat third density on another suitable planet.
   Meanwhile the yellow-ray sphere "will cease to be inhabited for some period" while fourth-density entities learn to shield their density from third. After that, third density may cycle there again (63.9, 11.6, 17.24, 10.6, 63.8, 62.29).
7. Guardians hail approaching entities "in the name of the One Creator", and only entities that find rare windows get through without permission. The light quarantine was intensified after genetic aid to Mars was seen as an infringement. It guards the free will of a third density that cannot fully recognize manipulation (12.5, 12.7, 21.8, 9.9, 16.1-16.2).
8. Permitted entry runs through the Council of Saturn (nine, backed by 24 Guardians), which weighs each request (6.25, 7.8, 7.9, 6.8). A servant social memory complex needs three things: aid that "can only be done among your peoples", consensus of the whole complex, and Council approval. Then "If it is approved, quarantine is lifted."
9. Ra gives these reasons higher beings do not simply appear: a mass landing would cost polarization by infringing free will (said of Orion, and called a gamble); Ra's own bodily appearance confused those it came to serve; and densities four to seven are invisible by choice (16.9, 23.6, 8.6, 13.20). What Ra does describe:
   - Council-permitted thought-form appearances in the skies;
   - service scaled to the call;
   - Wanderers: about 65 million in 1981, mostly sixth density. They take third-density genetics, forget, and go to the home vibration if the agreed mission is complete (7.12, 26.32, 7.1, 24.8, 12.26-12.28, 65.19, 70.15).
   Reading this as one general rule, "open presence infringes free will", is GUIDE synthesis.
10. Separately, "not yet in excess of 35,000" harvestable entities from other planets were incarnating with "a double body in activation", including many metal-bending children. They are not Wanderers and can consciously know fourth-density understandings (63.11-63.17, 65.19).
11. On your specific question, Ra does not address it. No passage says when or how higher-density beings may openly mingle with newly fourth-density beings. None says the quarantine ends at harvest, and none says whether Council approval still applies once Earth is fourth density; this was checked against every quarantine passage. The nearest material:
   - positive fourth density goes out to aid those who seek it, and fifth density sends groups "as instruments of light" (48.6);
   - Ra says "it may be said that the veil in fourth density is lifted", in an unrelated passage (87.25);
   - at the end of the second cycle a Confederation being appeared to about 150 harvestable third-density entities, spoke, and left (22.9-22.13).
   None of these is a rule for higher-density contact with Earth's new fourth density, so any timing or trigger in the simulation is design choice, not Ra.
12. On the higher self, Ra says (70.9, 36.2, 61.7, 70.11, 51.1):
   - it is "you in mid-sixth density";
   - it aids healing and life programming;
   - it keeps producing catalyst for less aware entities until a bias occurs;
   - it "does not manipulate its past selves" but "protects when possible and guides when asked";
   - it belongs to the first level watching over harvest.
   Ra never says it decides harvestability. Ra is inconsistent on whether the "totality" that chooses repetition or moving forward after harvest is the higher self (10.9, 51.1, 36.2).
