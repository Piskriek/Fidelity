## The owner's idea: "choice is what fourth density adds"

**Label: departs, with a nuance.**
- **Departs:** Ra puts the choice in third density (76.15, 76.16, 77.16), and the veils that make it possible are third-density (77.14).
- **The nuance:** choosing is not frozen at the end of third density. Ra speaks of continuous refining (76.16). About 2% move from negative to positive during fourth density (47.5), and a sixth-density negative entity can reorient (36.15) (RA; the generalisation is GUIDE).
- **What survives of the idea (GUIDE):** the planet's move into fourth density is when the third-density choice is tested at harvest (13.22, 13.23, 17.1), and fourth density is where the choice is lived.
- **What fourth density adds, by Ra:** understanding, compassion, transparency (selves not hidden), and the social memory complex.

**"Everything becomes 4"** is not addressed by Ra. Ra describes sevens and an octave (16.51).

## Does each density contain the earlier ones? (GUIDE, partly supported)

- Red is the foundation for all that is to come (40.3), and red strengthens each density (39.10).
- The fourth-density sphere coexists with first, second and third (63.8).
- Each entity contains all the densities and sub-densities (71.13).

Ra gives no explicit "each adds one" rule.

## A design note (mine, not Ra's; see Ra's caution at 78.30–78.31)

Ra's own description fits the v5 ladder better than the "choice = fourth" mapping drawn in chat:

| Ra's density | What Ra says it adds | v5 level |
|---|---|---|
| third | self-awareness and choice | L3: a gate that measures itself, or learns from its own errors without seeing the message (Casym, Cneed, S, E) |
| fourth | understanding, transparency, selves not hidden | L4: a gate that can *see where the message came from* and tell when it does not fit (R and RP can; E cannot) |
| fifth | wisdom balancing compassion; unrelieved compassion is folly | L5: always-open (B) is pure compassion, accepting all help; a gate passes only if it beats B by closing where help is wrong |
| sixth | unity, and the social memory complex | L6: sharing across models (a transplant), not tested in v5 |

This is an analogy to design tests with. It is not a claim that model levels are densities.
