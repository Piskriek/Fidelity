## Where the owner's pattern does appear in Ra

**Two to three, once.** Second-density beings are "self-aware mind/body complexes". On entering third density they become "mind/body/spirit complexes" (13.21). That one step matches the owner's "+1" (RA). It does not continue: beings harvested from fourth density are still mind/body/spirit complexes (7.16), so Ra describes no four-part being (GUIDE).

**Nine is in Ra, as three times three.** Before the veil this Logos used nine archetypes (79.14, confirmed at 79.15): mind, body and spirit, each working under matrix, potentiator and significator (79.17) (RA).

**After the veil: three sets of seven, plus one Choice.** "The sevens plus The Choice" (91.16) gives 7 + 7 + 7 + 1 = 22, and The Choice is "relatively fixed and single" (77.13). This system is specific to this Logos. Other Logoi use other numbers, down to two systems of five (91.14–91.17, 76.10) (RA).

**Choice belongs to third density.** Ra calls the choice "the axis upon which creation turns" (76.16). Polarization is required for harvest from third density, and the higher densities work from the polarity gained there (77.16) (RA).

**Reading (mine, not Ra's).** The owner's "three, then choice makes four" has a close structural echo in "three complexes, plus The Choice". But in Ra the choice is made in third density and carried into fourth, so it is not something fourth density adds.

## Other verified points

- **The three primal distortions** are free will, Logos or Love, and Light (15.21). Ra places them at creation, not in one density (GUIDE reading).
- **Nine:** the Council of Saturn sits as nine, backed by twenty-four Guardians (7.8, 7.9).
- **Three:** three is the minimum support group and the most efficient for this contact. The number is the questioner's, and Ra confirms it for this contact only (36.21, 36.22).
- **Seven:** Ra's plainest statement of a recurring number is seven, from the largest scale to the smallest (47.8).
- **Density and dimension** are used together early on (6.7, 16.25). "Dimensions" are also spoken of as infinite in number (13.8). Ra never defines the difference between them (GUIDE).
- **Distortion** is Ra's word for any differentiation from unity, and it is not pejorative. Only the Law of One is not a distortion (22.27, 27.7) (GUIDE reading of RA statements).

## A caution for the density-to-model mapping

Ra says the archetypes and the densities relate only "tangentially", with "no congruency" (78.30), and warns against insisting that structures line up one-to-one (78.31). The density-to-model table drawn in chat on 2026-11-01 is a working analogy for designing tests. It is not a claim that the model's levels are densities.
