# Mechanics map: from the owner's universe to the beings' world (2026-11-02)

**Status: a design document. Every row is a candidate, none is a ruling.** No row goes into the Plan until the owner approves it. Nothing here changes a locked file.

The owner asked (2026-11-02, their words): "have you done a map of the mechanics from my universe you want to use in yours? stuff like telepathy is a starting skill in the Sirius system where im originally from but on earth it depends cos there are so many diffrent entities. so lots to choose from with diffrent effects". This is that map. The beings' world is Four Valleys v1: four beings, one per valley, each sensing only its energy and health in 5 bands and the fruit in front of it, guessing what each choice will bring, choosing, and feeling surprise and relief. It cannot sense the season, the other valleys, or the rules.

A companion file, `WORLD_V2_DESIGN.md`, uses this map: the teacher-engine frame, which senses first, and the owner's decisions C-1 to C-6 as plain questions.

## How to read the map

**Where each mechanic comes from** (the first words of the "owner's frame" column):
- **Owner:** the owner's own words, quoted and dated. Their framings are one viewpoint, theirs, and are never judged here. Their account of where they come from is theirs; this map does not confirm or test it.
- **Plan:** the owner's design document (`FIDELITY_COMPLETE_BRS_V2.md`, with its Resume and Skill, canonical in GitHub). Parts of it were proposed by Argon or Opus; §8.4 to §8.6 record what the owner ruled.
- **MarbleGp:** the MarbleGp docs and `MARBLEGP_MECHANICS_REVIEW.md`.
- **Argon:** a note by Argon in the MarbleGp docs, not the owner's words.
- **Mine:** a candidate from this project's own probes; the word "kind" is kept for fruit kinds (a, b, c), and a being's starting senses are called its **sense profile** (the owner says "kind of entity"). It is not in the owner's universe; it is listed because an owner mechanic has no other form in this world.

**What it would be in the beings' world:** a **sense** (a new input), an **action** (a new thing a being does), a **port** (a channel between a being and something above or beside it) or a **world change** (the world's own rules change).

**Cost class:**
- **a** = new senses only (reading (a) of C-1). The world's rules are untouched; the minds get a new input, are retrained, and the three tests (E1, E2, E3) are re-run. **a+** = the sense reads something the world's snapshot does not hold yet, so the world must also record it (more state bytes; the dynamics are unchanged), and the frozen world tests and mutant checks are re-run.
- **b** = patterned world (reading (b)): the fruit gets structure at several scales. New world code, retraining, every test re-run.
- **c** = space (reading (c)): a ring of cells around each being, with a move action. A new world.
- **rule** = a new action or port beside the three (EAT, PASS, REST), such as an ask. It names its own cost: at least a+, plus a second ledger byte so the checks can regenerate the tokens.
- **run** = already running in the world today.
- **lens** = a lens or translation change only. No change to the world or the minds.
- **none** = no mapping into this world now (engine-side, or for a later stage).

**Evidence:** probe numbers are from `mind/runs/probes/PROBES.md` (a count model, on unscored worlds 40000–40019, under the nursery's scripted policy), plus one new probe added this round. They say how much a sense tells about the next answer, in the world. They do **not** say what a learner will pick up: the nursery's minds left 0.12 nats of in-reach memory headroom unused. Lower CE is more information; the base is 0.3176.

**Rulings that bear** (the owner's, all stay in force; "O" applies to every row and is not repeated):
- **P** felt pain is a higher-self choice, never a designer setting; intensity only goes down;
- **J** we don't judge: describe fit, not worth;
- **L** a lens never lies;
- **A** one side asks, the other permits;
- **C** news of how help landed returns (compassion);
- **F** the owner's framings are labelled as theirs;
- **D** durations only from measured logs;
- **O** no design ruling goes into the Plan without the owner's approval.

**Ra label:** follows / departs / partly / silent in the sessions checked, taken only from the verified lookups in `bridge/law_of_one/`. "L1" to "L9" mean the lookups: L1 densities and archetypes (`RA_DENSITIES_1`), L2 what each density adds (`RA_DENSITIES_2`), L3 harvest and contact (`RA_HARVEST_3`), L4 prayer (`RA_PRAYER_4`), L5 catalyst and helpers (`RA_CATALYST_5`), L6 group and density (`RA_GROUP_DENSITY_6`), L7 perception, higher self, teaching and safety (`RA_PERCEPTION_TEACHER_7`), L8 abilities by kind of entity (`RA_KINDS_8`, verified: 24 of 29 claims supported, 5 partly, 0 unsupported), L9 distortion, pain, fear, pull and push (`RA_FEAR_PAIN_9`, verified: 42 of 58 claims supported, 16 partly, 0 unsupported). "Plan §8.x" means the Plan's own labels, produced there by a guide and a reviewer and not re-fetched for this map. **nc** = not checked: no lookup covers it. All lookups read page summaries, so Ra's wording is approximate. A cell that says "silent" means silent in the sessions checked.
