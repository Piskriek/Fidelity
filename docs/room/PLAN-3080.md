How sure each figure is: **measured** means measured in your house on 05 Nov. **Checked** means checked tonight in our code, in a simulation, or in published documents. **Estimate** means nobody has measured it. **Unverified** means it is expected, but nobody has seen it on your machines.

## 1. What changes and why

Everything moves to the 3080 PC. WoW, the head tracker reading your phone, the depth model and the 3D page all run there; the laptop stays on only so Argon can work. On 05 Nov the laptop could not run WoW and the 3D together: one depth picture took 18.9 s and one face search 3.1 s. The PC, meanwhile, followed your phone at 26.8 pictures a second and made a depth picture in 104 to 189 ms (measured). With every part on one machine, the trip between the laptop and the PC and the laptop's 107 ms of picture-making both disappear, and one double-click starts everything. Tonight WoW goes onto the PC and Claude makes the start foolproof and the numbers trustworthy. Tomorrow you sit at the PC with the phone on top of the screen and judge the look, and the run's numbers come back to Claude to tune the speed.
