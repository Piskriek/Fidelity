## Q1. Sleep versus between lives

**[RA]**
- Ra does not confine contact to the between-life review. Dreaming is "communication through the veil of the unconscious mind" and the conscious mind (86.7). For entities blocked in the lower centres it repeats "recent catalyst" and deeper blockages, giving the waking mind "clues" (86.7). Usually the dream is "muddied, muddled, and quickly lost" (86.10).
- The dream is self-authored. Asked whether the higher self designs it, Ra said the complex "is responsible for this activity" (86.8) and agreed the subconscious writes the script (86.9).
- Higher-self contact in sleep is described only for adepts, who may call on guides and "the higher self in space/time analog" as it enters sleeping consciousness (86.7).
- Dreaming heals: "a finely wrought and excellently fashioned bridge from conscious to unconscious" (86.12). It works through time/space, whose equivalent duration is "far greater" than the brief span it seems to take (86.15).
- The veil is "the single door against which the higher self must stand awaiting entry", and a "primary cause of the value of dreams" (83.3).
- The full review is between lives. Its value is "the testing as opposed to the studying", since in life attention goes "almost inevitably" to detail (83.7). It is "done entirely by the higher self" until the entity is conscious of the process, then the entity will "consciously take part in all decisions" (71.6).
- Limits on lifting the veil. A Wanderer can remember "what it is and why it is upon the planetary sphere", but going further "would be an infringement" (65.19). Ra's own contact needed the forgetting penetrated, or the Law of Confusion would bar it (36.19, 36.20). Wanderers do so through "disciplined meditation and working" (63.17).
- Confederation contact with Wanderers often happens in sleep, avoids "causing fear" (53.12), and follows the subject's "subconscious expectations" (53.13). Only Wanderers get it, since "The infringement upon free will is greatly undesired" (53.7).
- Dreams are an attack route: "through the mechanism of images and dreams" negative concepts can be offered (46.2).
- Stakes. Ra says the pre-veil, non-complex mind had "no pain terribly frightening" (82.22). Ra called "quite reasonable" the analogy of a simulator "bolted to the ground" (83.8).

**[COMMON]** (unverified) Community readers often say the higher self and guides meet the sleeper freely each night. Ra's explicit statement covers adepts only (86.7).

**[GUIDE]** Ra supports a partial dream stage, not a memory restore. Replay, clues and healing match 86.7, 86.12 and 86.15. Nightly recall of the chosen game goes beyond Ra and cuts against Ra's stakes argument (82.22, 83.8).
*§8.5 rule.* A dream stage may replay recent catalyst, rebalance and give symbolic clues, authored by the entity's own subconscious. A direct higher-self cue is reserved for adept-level entities. Full restoration of the game belongs to the between-life review. Dream content must not be an injection channel for outside agents.

## Q2. Thresholds and timescales

**[RA]**
- Ra gives no repeat counts. The loop is open-ended: "There is no lack of space/time in which this catalyst may work", and the entity "proceeds until catalyst strikes it" and forms a bias (46.16). For less aware entities the higher self "continues producing catalyst until a bias occurs" (61.7). Catalyst gone sour draws "additional catalyst" (34.6).
- Unused catalyst will "filter through to the body complex" (66.34; also 61.7). Ra states no interval, only that its use needs "a period of consistent learn/teaching" (42.9).
- The one explicit cap is on intensity: an entity should "not receive more experience in more intensity than it may bear" (22.6). It applies "only in effect upon an individual level", not to planetary or social complexes (22.6). The tool is shortening the incarnation, removing the entity for healing and evaluation (22.6).
- Life-span figures: about 900 years at the start, about 700 after the first 25,000-year cycle (20.12, 20.14), then typically 35 to 40 (22.4). Ra ties shortening to rejected lessons (22.5) and to catalyst not assimilated "with the proper rhythm and increment" (76.21).
- Shortening has costs. "A much longer incarnation in your space/time continuum is very helpful" for finishing intensive work (22.6). The present span leaves most entities in "perpetual childhood" (20.25). Without progress, "those conditions which grant progress are gradually lost" (20.17).
- Persistence across lives. Entities carry "previous biases created in other life experiences" (36.14). Karma stops by forgiveness at any point (34.5). The longest case is Maldek, whose trauma dates from about 705,000 years ago (10.1), with "a few who have not yet alleviated" it (10.4).
- Between lives the gap lengthens as entities develop, to "give appropriate attention to the review and the healing" (21.9). No durations are given. Re-offering has no frequency, only a metaphor: cards "dealt and re-dealt and re-dealt continuously" (50.7).
- Cadences Ra does give: one spiritual cycle of "approximately eighteen of your diurnal cycles" (61.3), "interesting but not critical" (64.13). Its ratios are distributions: 51% and 95% thresholds (17.32, 17.33), 54% self-choosing in 1981 (21.11), about 10/60/30 harvest outcomes (65.13).
- Help. "In time of harvest there are always harvesters" (51.1). Otherwise the Confederation is "at the call" of those on Earth (60.27), "Service is only possible to the extent it is requested" (89.30), and Ra advised dropping a "desire to serve where no service is requested" (67.27). The higher self "protects when possible and guides when asked" (70.11).

**[GUIDE]** The thresholds are your parameters, but Ra fixes the shape. A loop is defined by no change of bias after repeated offers, not by elapsed time. The response is a per-entity intensity cap (22.6) whose exit is an early end of incarnation into review and healing, not deletion. Its cost to newcomers (20.25) should stay visible. Health state is Ra's gauge for unused catalyst (66.34).
*§8.5 rule.* A loop-break fires when the same catalyst has been re-offered N times without a bias change (N is an owner parameter, not from Ra). The response is a per-entity cap and, if needed, an early return to review. The throttle never acts at world or cohort level. Active help beyond that waits for a request, except harvest-time and review-time help.

## Q3. Entities without a soul record

**[RA]**
- Ra separates second-density "mind/body complexes" from third-density "mind/body/spirit complexes" (13.21). Animals, trees and places can individualize by being "capable of giving and receiving enough love" (19.2).
- Investment. Third-density beings "invest or clothe some second-density beings with self-awareness", via pets and via practices that "personify and send love to various natural second-density beings in their group form" (14.1). The commonest case is "the so-called pet", whose mind/body complex then "does not return unto the undifferentiated consciousness of that species" (20.3). Without investment "The process takes longer" (14.2). A cat's investment was "the greatest catalyst in this entity's progress" (30.13).
- Second-density character is "the striving towards light and growth" (13.18). Its self-service includes tribe or pack, "not seen in second density as separation of self and other-self" (19.15).
- Pain and choice. Ra gives no direct statement on whether second-density beings suffer or consent. Indirect hints: a cat's discomfort "is able to largely ignore" (104.7), and Orion terror aims to make humans feel "the feelings of an advanced second-density being such as a laboratory animal" (53.14). Ra called a questioner's account of base catalyst for mind/body complexes "partially correct" (54.17), naming the sub-Logos as source of the survival-level base. I found no statement that a higher self programs catalyst for them.
- Automatic incarnation concerns individuated third-density selves. They are a "newly individuated consciousness complex" with "small need to review or to heal" (21.9). Their programming is "a partially randomized, system of learnings" (92.11). Local beings handle their patterns: "You may call these beings angelic if you prefer" (48.8). (My inference: they have a record and little say.)
- Group and mass. The planetary mind is "a repository of biases remembered by the mind/body/spirit complexes", formed by choices "in freedom of will" (91.6, 91.7).
- Social memory complexes form "when its entire group of entities are of one orientation or seeking" (11.17). Ra speaks as "an individualized portion of consciousness" (17.13). Members still meet catalyst: fourth density uses "mental and spiritual pain" (43.9).
- Authored figures. Complexes project thought-form figures shaped by the subject's expectations, with "as little distortion towards alarm or discomfort as is allowable" (53.13), "subjective" experiences (53.14).
- Gap. Ra never addresses simulated or humanlike beings lacking a self. Self-conscious behavior marks third density (90.10: "behavior indicating self-consciousness").

**[COMMON]** (unverified) "Group soul" is community shorthand. A keyword search found no such phrase in the sessions; Ra's term is "undifferentiated consciousness of that species" (20.3).

**[GUIDE]** Cohort agents resemble Ra's undifferentiated species consciousness or shared racial mind: pools, not individuals. Ra still credits second-density beings with growth, fear and response to love, so it gives no licence to treat having no record as having no experience. Sustained individual attention is Ra's route to individuation (14.1, 20.3, 30.13).
*§8.5 rule.* Cohort agents get the gentlest tier: no engineered suffering, no fear mechanics, shared-pool catalyst only. Promote an agent to a soul record when it shows self-consciousness (90.10) or receives sustained individual investment. Any entity that receives programmed catalyst needs a record, automatic incarnators included (21.9, 92.11). Authored figures aimed at a soul-bearing entity follow its expectations and avoid alarm (53.12, 53.13).
