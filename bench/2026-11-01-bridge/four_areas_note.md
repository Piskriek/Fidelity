## 1. In one paragraph

**No new source of energy.** None of the four areas adds a new source of energy. Every real counterpart found turns out, again, to convert energy from a supply that can be metered, so the bridge's main rule stands. What these areas add is exactness and a few tests.

**Sacred geometry,** with its meanings set aside, gives four exact facts the gateway can use:

They give a working natural example of a gated port from a star to a planet: field direction and tilt scale the throughput, and the entry points drift [published; the mapping onto the bridge is metaphor and design].

**The pyramid scans** add a method rather than a mechanism. Muography finds hidden voids by comparing a book of expected particle counts with a book of measured counts [measured]. The satellite-radar claims had no second method behind them, and they did not survive: the method paper was retracted, and the Khafre claims were never peer-reviewed [published retraction].

**Ra's silences** turn out to be mostly a stated policy: principles are given, and operating specifics are withheld under free will. Many topics people think Ra avoided were never asked about [RA; GUIDE; the absence rests on keyword searches].

**Harvest.** Ra's one real contribution to the open harvest question is that harvest is a read-out of an entity's state, not a flow of energy (51.1) [RA; the mapping is GUIDE]. ## 2. The four areas

### 2.1 Sacred geometry

**What is real**
- **Platonic solids.** Exactly five regular convex solids exist, because the angles meeting at each corner must leave a gap: (p−2)(q−2) < 4 [established]. Virus shells, the C60 molecule and Shechtman's quasicrystals really do use icosahedral symmetry [established].
- **The Flower of Life.** Its circle centres form the triangular (hexagonal) lattice. The vesica's height-to-width ratio is √3 ≈ 1.732 [established; recomputed, model]. This lattice is the proven densest packing of circles in a plane. Stacked in layers, it gives the densest packing of spheres in space (Thue; Hales 2005 and 2017) [established].
- **The torus.**
  - Among closed two-sided surfaces, only the torus can carry a flow with no zero anywhere (the Poincaré–Hopf theorem) [established]. This is why magnetic confinement uses toroidal surfaces.
  - Grid-cell activity in the brain lies on a torus (Gardner et al., Nature 2022) [measured]. That torus lives in the space of neural states; it is not a physical donut.
- **Linked circles.**
  - Helicity, the quantity the bridge's port meters, equals the total linking of field lines (Moffatt 1969) [established].
  - In the Hopf fibration every pair of circles links exactly once [established; checked by model].
  - Knotted vortex loops have been made in water (Kleckner and Irvine 2013) [measured].
- **The cube roots of one.**
  - The lattice points are exactly the numbers m + n·a, where a is a turn of 120°. In power engineering, a is Fortescue's three-phase rotation operator.
  - Because 1 + a + a² = 0, three balanced phases cancel, and three waves at 120° build a hexagon [established].
  - Because a³ = 1, the 3rd, 6th and 9th harmonics add instead of cancelling, and they do not rotate. Engineers call them the zero-sequence family [established].
  - Hexagons in convection cells and in grid-cell firing come from this three-wave rule [firing pattern measured; mechanism published, Sorscher et al. 2023].
  - This turns the earlier 3-6-9 link into an identity rather than numerology.
- **Hexagon nets.**
  - Take a closed net of hexagons and pentagons with three faces meeting at every corner. It has exactly 12 pentagons on a sphere-like surface and none on a flat torus [established].
  - A real round torus curves outwards on its outside and inwards on its inside. An even hexagon layout there needs about 12 pentagons outside and 12 heptagons inside, or stretched cells [model].
- **The golden ratio φ.** φ is the number hardest to approximate by fractions (Hurwitz) [established]. This "anti-resonance" is why it appears in:
  - plant spirals (Douady and Couder 1992) [published]. In sunflowers, 565 of 768 spiral counts were Fibonacci numbers, and 136 had no Fibonacci structure (Swinton and Ochu 2016) [measured];
  - the last orbit to break up in a standard model of chaos, at k ≈ 0.9716 [published];
  - one measured quantum spectrum (Coldea et al. 2010) [measured, one system only].
- **The star tetrahedron.** It is a tetrahedron plus its mirror image through the centre. Its 8 corners are those of a cube, its core is an octahedron, and seen down a three-fold axis it is a hexagram [established; model check]. Separately, any back-and-forth oscillation is exactly two counter-rotating halves. This is the textbook account of single-phase motors [established].

**What does not survive**

  - It holds exact drawings of the tetrahedron, cube and octahedron.
  - The icosahedron and dodecahedron need golden-ratio proportions. No construction from its 13 points can produce them, because φ² cannot be written as a + b√3 [model plus exact algebra].

  - The name is modern and comes from the New Age movement [established; secondary source].
  - The oldest known overlapping-circles pattern is Assyrian, from the 7th–6th century BCE [established; secondary source].

  - Chance matches come easily. With 10 unrelated lengths and a ±2% tolerance, some pair looks "golden" 60% of the time. That falls to 37% at ±1%, and to 18% with only 5 lengths [model; the numbers depend on the assumptions].

### 2.2 Ley lines and Earth grids

**What is real**
- **Watkins's idea.** Alfred Watkins (1922, 1925) proposed that old sites lie on straight trackways laid out by sight. He claimed no energy [history; secondary sources].
- **Real straight features**, made for other reasons, do exist: Roman roads, the Nazca lines, and the Inca ceque lines radiating from Cusco [established or published].
- **Some sites do sit on real features:**
  - faults that channel water and gas: Delphi [published; the gas is debated], and the Hierapolis "gate to hell", with CO2 up to 91% in the grotto [measured];
  - faults that produce earthquake lights: 63 of 65 cases lay near steep faults, though such faults produce only about 5% of earthquakes (Thériault et al. 2014) [published];
  - the sky: Stonehenge's solstice axis [established];
  - footpaths: Nazca figures lie on average about 34–43 m from walking trails [published].
- **The Earth's own lines are real, but they move, and outside forces drive them.**
  - The north magnetic pole drifted about 36 km in 2025 [measured].
  - The great storm of 1989 induced ground electric fields of about 19–22 V/km [published; modelled from magnetometer data].
  - Birkeland currents flow along field lines into the polar regions. They total about 2.7 million amperes in quiet times and 5.2 million in disturbed times [measured].
  - The global electric circuit runs at about 250 kV and 0.25–1 GW [established; a range].
  - The cavity between the ground and the ionosphere rings at 7.83, 14.1, 20.3 Hz and higher. About 50 lightning flashes a second keep it ringing. It is leaky, with a quality factor Q of about 5 [measured; Q is model].
- **The Sun-to-Earth port.**
  - Solar-wind energy enters mainly where the solar wind's magnetic field reconnects with Earth's field at the magnetopause.
  - Field direction scales how much gets in. One MHD simulation finds about 3 times more total energy when the solar wind's field points south than when it points north (4.95% against 1.71%) [published]. For the energy that drives storms, the factor is 30 to 100 [published].
  - Earth's tilt through the year modulates the coupling [published]. Which tilt mechanism dominates is contested.
- **What goes up from Earth.** All of these are losses that nothing collects:
  - oxygen ions, about 0.03–3 kg/s [model, from a conference abstract];
  - radio from the aurora, at most about 10⁹ W, a few billionths of the sunlight Earth intercepts [published plus model];
  - by far the largest, infrared heat, about 1.2 × 10¹⁷ W [established].

**Meaning layered on it**
- The "earth energy" flowing along leys was added after Watkins: Tony Wedd's UFO-guidance idea (1961) and John Michell's dragon lines (1969) [history].
- Planetary crystal grids (Goncharov et al. 1973; Becker and Hagens) [unsupported].
- Oracles, water gods and ancestors were layered on real faults and springs.

**What does not survive**
- *Energy flowing along leys* [unsupported].
- *Dowsing* [unsupported: controlled tests were negative or are contested].
- *Radiation at sites forming lines* [untested]. The Dragon Project found no consistent pattern [secondary source].
- *"Leys line up more often than chance"* [unsupported].
  - A 40 × 40 km map of England holds about 193 open Church of England churches on average. With a 50 m-wide strip, pure chance gives about 175 sets of four and about 10 sets of five churches in a line [model].
  - Clustering like that of real villages raises those counts 1.5 to 2 times [model].
  - Statisticians reached the same verdict (Broadbent 1980; Williamson and Bellamy 1983, via secondary sources) [published].
  - The best pro-ley result, computer runs on Land's End stones in 1976, is unresolved because of how the stones were chosen.
- *"The word 'ley' survives in place names."* It comes from Old English *leah*, "clearing". The track meaning is Watkins's own coinage of 1922 [established]. So the place names count against leys.
- I found no published study that places ancient sites on magnetic, telluric (ground-current), Birkeland or Schumann lines [not found by searching].
- *Correction to an earlier bridge link* [metaphor].
  - The magnetopause "portal" is not an on/off switch; field direction scales it.
  - It also matches Ra's quarantine "windows" poorly. Flux transfer events (bursts of reconnection) recur about every 8 minutes when the field points south (Rijnbeek et al. 1984) [published]. Ra describes those windows as rare and unpredictable (12.7) [RA, paraphrased].

**Cheapest test.** A desk test:
1. Take one county's list of scheduled prehistoric monuments.
2. Before looking, fix the strip width and the minimum number of points per line.
3. Count the alignments.
4. Compare the count with 1,000 random sets that keep the real clustering.

Real leys would need a clear excess at five points or more.

### 2.3 The pyramid scans

**What is real**
- **The Big Void** [measured].
  - A void at least 30 m long lies above the Grand Gallery of Khufu's pyramid. Three kinds of muon detector saw it (Morishima et al., Nature 2017).
  - The film detectors reached 13.7σ and 12.7σ. The gas detectors reached 5.8σ for the void and 8.4σ for the known Grand Gallery, which served as a built-in check.
  - Nobody has entered the void, and no access is known. Its purpose is unknown, and it may be a gap left during construction.
  - I found no new results for 2025–2026 [searches only].
- **The North Face Corridor** [measured].
  - It is about 9 m long and 2 × 2 m in cross-section. Muons found it (Nature Communications 2023), and radar and ultrasound from the same consortium confirmed it.
  - A 5 mm endoscope then filmed it; the results were released in March 2023.
  - It is most likely a space that relieves the weight above the entrance.
- **Menkaure's pyramid** shows two small air-filled anomalies behind its east face [published, preliminary]. That they mark a second entrance is a hypothesis.
- **How muography works.**
  - Cosmic-ray muons above 1 GeV arrive from overhead at about 70 per m² per second per steradian [established].
  - They carry about 7 × 10⁻⁸ W/m², about 5 × 10⁻¹¹ of sunlight. About 1% get through 100 m of limestone [model; the 1% is also the paper's figure].
- **Dating.**
  - The Diary of Merer papyri log limestone deliveries in Khufu's year 27 [established].
  - Khufu's cartouches are painted in relieving chambers that stayed sealed until 1837 [established].
  - Radiocarbon dates on the mortar run a few hundred years older than the historical date [published].
  - Together these put the pyramid at about 4,500–4,800 years old.
- **Resonance.**
  - The King's Chamber rings at its room modes: 16.4, 29.4 and 32.8 Hz, and 105 modes below 130 Hz [model]. Near 117 Hz there is a mode about every 0.7 Hz, so the often-quoted 117 Hz is nothing special [model].
  - A published computer model shows the pyramid concentrating long radio waves (200–600 m wavelength) into its chambers and base (Balezin et al. 2018) [published model].
  - Both effects redistribute energy that arrives from outside. Neither creates any.

**Meaning layered on it**
- "Hidden chamber", "hall of records", "power plant", "healing frequency".
- Muography measures only one thing: less rock than expected along certain lines of sight.

**What does not survive**
- *"Shafts 600 m to 2 km under Khafre's pyramid" (2025).*
  - The claims were never peer-reviewed, and no permits were issued. Radar specialists rejected them.
  - The team's only peer-reviewed paper on the method, a 2022 paper about Khufu, was retracted on 10 August 2026 for serious flaws in its method and statistics. No misconduct was alleged, and the authors disagree [published retraction].
  - Newsweek's fact check rated the 2026 "second Sphinx" claim false [unsupported].
  - On the physics, the decisive point: seismic waves at 1–20 Hz have wavelengths of 75–3,000 m in rock, far too coarse to resolve spiral shafts. And the method was never tested on a structure whose interior is known [model].
  - The vibration budget alone is marginal rather than hopeless [model; it depends on the assumed amplitudes].
- **Pyramid power.**
  - Small tests in 1973 and one TV test found no effect on razor blades, flowers or food [measured, weak].
  - One Indian lab reported lower stress markers in rats kept under a wooden pyramid than in a cubic box (Bhat et al. 2003, 2007) [published]. But there was one enclosure per group, the box held three times the air, blinding was not reported, and I found no independent replication. The result is weak and not established.
  - No mechanism is supported [unsupported].
- *Ra's date.* Ra says the Great Pyramid came first, formed about 6,000 years before the sessions (23.6) [RA, paraphrased]. The record says about 4,500–4,800 years, with step pyramids built earlier [established]. The gap is roughly 1,400 years.
- *Ra's numbers as independent confirmation.*
  - The 76°18′ apex angle and the one-sixth-height point were the questioner's figures. The questioner asked whether the 76° 18′ apex angle was a critical angle (55.15, questioner). Ra replied that for the intended healing work the angle is appropriate (55.15) [RA, paraphrased]. Of the one-sixth point, Ra said the questioner's calculations were substantially correct (55.10) [RA, paraphrased]. Both match Giza, but neither is independent.
  - Ra's own number is 1.16: Ra says the proportion should be the 1.16 the questioner may observe (57.22) [RA, paraphrased]. The questioner restated it as the sum of the four base sides being 1.16 of the pyramid's height (57.23, questioner), and Ra confirmed this (57.23) [RA, paraphrased]. That gives a needle-like apex of about 16.5°. Giza's perimeter is 6.28 times its height [model].
  - For the same small pyramid placed under the head, Ra also calls the Giza pyramid an excellent model for energy through the apex angle (66.25) [RA, paraphrased]. That is an inconsistent recommendation rather than a flat contradiction, because 1.16 does fit Ra's statement that the lesser angle may be any angle under 70° (57.32) [RA, paraphrased].
  - 1.16 reads the same in all three transcript versions, so it is not a transcription slip [checked in the geometry check run]. Ra itself asked to be checked on numbers (57.33, see section 3).
- *"Muography is evidence about harvest going up."* This has the direction backwards. In muography the lower level pulls information out of a stream coming down, and nothing goes up [at most a metaphor]. Earth's magnetic gate also plays no part in these counts: the muons' parent particles carry roughly 50 times the energy below which the gate turns particles away [model].

**Cheapest test.** The pyramid claim that bears on the bridge is that a shape by itself does something to living bodies. The cheapest real test is a blinded replication:

**Cheapest test.** A ledger audit on public data:

The output must not exceed the input. Any claimed free-energy device should meet the standard the Steorn jury applied: meter every input independently, close the loop, and run blinded controls.

## 4. Bridge additions

Every item below is [design]: a proposal for the owner to accept, change or drop. Each comes with a simulation test that could fail.

     - zero for two equal counter-rotating halves at any offset;
     - zero on average for two perpendicular drives at unequal frequencies, because the cross term averages out [established mathematics].
