# Pre-registration: the owner's three-place check on pairs with "no real third"

Written 2026-11-01, before any agent in this test ran.

## Why

The watchers (Haiku and Gemini challengers, two strict judges each) confirmed nine pairs where the judges found no real third. All nine are mathematics: definitions, numbers, sets, graphs, a linear program. Every physical pair they tried dissolved, because a real third turned up (a field, a medium, an environment).

The owner's reply was: "you have to check what is surrounding it and what is inside it and the third thing", and "everything is also a bridge/conduit". The judges asked a single question: does naming a third change the verdict? They never searched place by place.

## The check

Each pair (A, B, stated relation) is searched in three places:

- **Surrounding:** the container, context, frame, medium or system the pair sits in.
- **Inside:** what A and B are made of, their parts or structure.
- **Bridge:** what carries or conducts the relation between A and B. This is something other than A, B and the bare relation. Each thing is also treated as a conduit between its own surrounding and its inside.

The "bridge" place is my reading of the owner's "the third thing". The owner has not confirmed it.

Three place-checkers (Sonnet) search the places, one place each, and each returns the strongest candidate in its place for every pair. Three judges grade each candidate: Opus, Sonnet and Haiku. The judges see only the definitions below. They do not see this file, the hypotheses or which pairs are controls. The grades are:

- **VARIABLE:** a real third that, varied while A and B stay as stated, changes the stated relation.
- **FIXED:** a real third the relation rests on, which cannot vary without redefining A or B, because it is held fixed by definition.
- **NONE:** nothing real in this place. The candidate only restates A, B or the relation, or the relation does not depend on it.

A candidate takes the grade at least two of the three judges give it. If all three judges disagree, it is UNSETTLED.

## Predictions, locked

- **P1 (control check):** each of the three physical controls has a VARIABLE third in at least one place. If any control fails, the test is broken and nothing below is read.
- **P2 (the owner's claim, made testable):** each of the nine mathematical pairs has at least one third graded FIXED or VARIABLE in at least one place. A pair whose candidates all come out NONE in all three places is a counterexample that survives the owner's check.
- **P3 (my bridge hypothesis):** in the mathematical pairs, the thirds are FIXED rather than VARIABLE. Counted per pair, by the pair's best grade, at least 6 of the 9 have no VARIABLE third. Under this hypothesis, mathematics holds its third fixed by definition, and the earlier judges' test, which works by varying, cannot see a third that does not vary.
- **P4 (the opposite of P3):** if 5 or more of the 9 mathematical pairs have a VARIABLE third, P3 fails. The earlier "no third" verdicts were then a search failure, which matches the owner's reading.

## Limits, stated in advance

- Every checker is told to find a third, so it will propose something. The NONE grade and the three judges exist to catch thirds that are forced.
- All the judges are Claude models from one vendor. An outside-vendor judge (Gemini) may be added afterward. It is labelled as added.
- What a "third" is remains a definitional choice. FIXED is counted as real here. If the owner rules that only a varying third is real, read P3's counts in place of P2's.
