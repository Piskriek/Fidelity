1. Make the R2 "Numbers" and "State (unit+range)" the only mandatory card fields; make Operators/Invariants/Evidence optional ("write only when a check needs it").
2. In Check 5, add the explicit sub-checks "(i) counts in headings vs. items listed, (ii) sum of weights per formula, (iii) ledger totals vs. stated totals, (iv) caps × example sizes vs. size budgets." Those four caught about a third of findings here.
3. Add a Check 9 "Reachability": for every clamp or cap, do the plan's own named values (examples, materials, extremes) lie inside it? (#13 would be found directly.)
4. Add a "Floating-point equality" line to Check 2 (=== on computed sums).
5. Regrow: require the regrowth to be written by a fresh reader (subagent) who has only the card, otherwise "without re-reading" cannot be honoured. Alternatively drop the "without re-reading" claim and say "derive the formula yourself, then diff".
6. Replace the "stop when a sweep adds nothing" rule with "stop when a sweep adds no H/M findings, or after N sweeps; report remaining Low items as unconverged."
7. Remove the SQ-terms parenthetical, or define the terms.
8. Add to Output a mandatory "Computed by code" column or tag, so unsupported arithmetic claims by the auditor are distinguished from verified ones.
