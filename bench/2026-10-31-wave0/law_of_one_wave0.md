# Law of One for Wave 0: energy (B100), memory (B103), the soul cartridge (B126)

Source: Ra sessions at lawofone.info only. Method as before: I searched all 106 pages, read each cited passage in full, and a script checked every quotation against the cited session.question. ## Q1. Energy (B100)

**[RA]**
- Source. Unity has "a potential and kinetic"; the potential is intelligent infinity, and "Tapping this potential will yield work", which Ra calls "intelligent energy" (27.5). "The origin of all energy is the action of free will upon love" and its nature is light (54.27). Intelligent infinity's rhythm beats outward until "all is coalesced" (27.6).
- Local law. Each solar system has "its own, shall we say, local coordinate system of illusory natural laws" (13.13). Ra says the Logos fixes the ways of intelligent energy and the sub-Logos works within them (41.4).
- Stars. The sun is physically a body "undergoing the processes of fusion and radiating heat and light" (41.4).
- Conservation. I found no Ra statement that energy is conserved or not conserved. "Zero point" appears once, in a black-hole simile for "infinitely great mass" (29.18). It is not a vacuum-energy source. Ra never links the phrase to free energy.
- Access conditions. Tapping intelligent energy through crystals needs "a certain balance" in the user, set per entity (29.27), and "the sympathetic vibration in balanced state accompanying the will to serve" (29.29). The user "charges the regularized physical crystal with this seeking" (29.30). Uncontrolled access also occurs, as a "random hole or gateway" in the spirit field (6.1).
- Crystals and pyramids. Crystal uses include "healing, for power, and even for the development of life-forms" (29.31). Pyramids are collectors for "light energy which is omni-present", not Earth energy (58.12). "These shapes are dangerous" when misused or placed wrongly (56.3).
- Atlantis. Trainees began "to use crystal powers for those things other than healing" (22.23). Later conflict used "nuclear bombs and other crystal weapons" (24.4).
- Free energy. Ra says Tesla received Confederation information and aimed to give the planet "the infinite energy of the planetary sphere for use in lighting and power" (11.26). The product would be "no need to find the necessary emolument for payment" for energy, and leisure (11.28). Scope: this states his aim, not that a device worked. Ra says information offered to Einstein "became perverted", and Tesla's was also experimented with for destruction (26.20). A nuclear-power equation becoming a weapon basis "was not intended and was not given" (17.8).
- Withholding. Ra says current technology could resolve "each and every limitation", but those with power-distortions cause "these solutions to be withheld" until they are so needed that power grows (8.11). Scope: the question concerned craft and transport energy. Ra also says Earth craft run on "the field of electromagnetic energy which polarizes the Earth sphere" (8.7).
- Ra declined crystal instruction because of "a tendency to use peaceful sources of power for disharmonious reasons" (29.31). Matter-to-energy weapons can interrupt the passage after death (26.22).
- Ra's own craft is "that thought-form upon which our concentration may function as motivator" (51.4). Ra rates manipulating the outer world as "far, far less of an aid to personal evolution" than inner discipline (52.2).

**[COMMON]** (unverified) Free-energy suppression stories are widely told. Ra supports only the withholding in 8.11.

**[GUIDE]** Ra supports two layers. Stars are the primary source of ordinary physics (41.4). An unlimited potential exists but is reached only through a gateway whose use depends on the user's state, not on hardware alone (29.27 to 29.30). Ra is silent on conservation, so that choice is free.
*Rule.* Ordinary physics is strictly conserved, with stars as the only primary source. Any declared infinite source is a gateway: an entity may draw on it only above its own balance threshold, using a charged object that is inert otherwise. The cost is the owner's decision, since Ra names none. Draws are logged, and misuse has world consequences (22.23, 24.4, 29.31).

## Q2. Memory (B103)

**[RA]**
- Within life, ordinary mental activity is "as much of a surface illusion as is the chemical body complex" (30.4). In the incarnation "over-attention is almost inevitably placed upon detail" (83.7). Dreams repeat "recent catalyst" and give the waking mind "clues" (86.7).
- What persists. At death "nothing whatever of importance is lost", leaving "pure distillation of emotions and biases or distortions and wisdoms" (30.4). The review is "not that testing which involves the correct memorization of many details" but "an attitude or complex of attitudes which bias the consciousness" (83.7). The review has the whole life available "as a book or record" (83.7) and spends time to "review and re-review the biases and learn/teachings" (71.6).
- Across lives. A new infant's significant self is "the harvest of biases of all previous incarnational experiences" (92.18). Experiences are "recorded" in the mind and body (92.18). Positive or negative events become "recorded as productive of biases" that may run opposite to their face value (97.16). Biases from "other life experiences" feed later choices (36.14). Prior programming can leave "a pattern or inertia" that a later life cannot break (34.17). Karma "may be called inertia" until forgiveness brakes it (34.4, 34.5).
- Through the veil. The difference between veiled and unveiled entities is "a forgetting within the deeper mind" (105.19), so the memory is kept in the deeper mind but not available (my reading). A Wanderer's memory is "within the infant's undeveloped mind complex" yet cannot be used (66.6). One strong memory persisted, "integrated into the unconscious", through "its extreme desire to remember" (27.1). Scope: that is one case.
- Ra gives no slot counts, no ranking of memories by intensity, and no statement that vivid events are kept while ordinary ones fade. Its retention criteria are the distilled bias and the unresolved pattern.

**[GUIDE]** Neither option matches Ra. Option B, 32 recent episodes, fits the idea that detail fades. Option A's four permanent intense episodes is closer in spirit, but Ra's persistent unit is the bias that remains after episodes fade, not the event.
*Rule.* Keep the 32-slot recency window for episodic detail. Update a separate distilled bias record after every episode, and carry it with the entity. Pin an episode beyond the window only while its catalyst is unresolved, and release it when the entity forgives or resolves it (34.4, 34.5). The between-session review reads the full window and writes the distillation; the cartridge does not keep the window.

## Q3. The soul cartridge (B126)

**[RA]**
- The higher self has "the totality of experiences which have been collected by an entity" (36.2). The totality is "a nebulous collection of all that may occur held in understanding", and the higher self can communicate "during the discarnate part of a cycle of rebirth" or, "if the proper pathways or channels through the roots of mind are opened", during incarnation (36.10). Ra says the late-sixth-density self uses its "total living bank or memory of experienced thoughts and actions" (37.6).
- Left behind: the chemical body and much surface mental activity, "as much of a surface illusion as is the chemical body complex" (30.4). Continued: a denser, "intelligently informed" body (30.3). Incarnation entry needs "the indigo-ray or etheric body for this is the form maker" (48.7), and in review the entity is "placed by the form-maker and higher self" (71.6).
- "Akashic" names two things: a view of the planet's potential social memory (14.32) and the planetary or racial mind, "a repository of biases remembered by the mind/body/spirit complexes" (91.6, 91.7), a shared store.

*Rule.* The cartridge carries distilled biases, polarity, unresolved-pattern flags, healing debt owed, and chosen catalyst. Vitality and strain are rebuilt at birth from the carried flags and pre-incarnative limits. The cartridge never stores the episode window.
