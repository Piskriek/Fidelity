# Wave 0 design notes

**1. Memory (B103)**
Rule: Keep the four episodes with the highest intensity `ex` permanently.
Ra basis: Departs from Ra. Ra gives no intensity ranking and no slot count. What persists is the distilled bias: at death "nothing whatever of importance is lost", leaving "pure distillation of emotions and biases" (30.4; see also 92.18).

**2. Gateway coupling (B100)**
Rule: Coupling coefficient C = 0.96, an owner-set design value.
Ra basis: Ra is silent on efficiency. It supports a gateway where access needs "a certain balance" in the user (6.1; 29.27), which the design follows.
Model basis: None. The toroid model does not measure C. Its 96.47% is the energy fraction released when two counter-spun toroids relax at b = 0.9 (balanced toroids release 32.86%). That is conversion inside the toroid, not transfer through the gateway. Plan §8.6 Q1 puts any inflow behind the gateway.

**3. Soul line (B126)**
Description: The soul line `ms` carries eight fields: `s` (four base-nature floats: pxd, vtx, lx, aq), `arch` (mind, body and spirit archetype weights), `pol` (sto01 and sts01 polarity), `vital` (5 to 100), `strain` (0 to 500), `debt` (recovery debt owed, 0 to 64), `doses` (0 to 7) and `hp` (0 to 1).
Ra basis: `arch` and `pol` follow Ra (76.10, 67.30; 85.9). `debt` follows Ra in kind (21.9, 69.6). `s` is design (29.26). `vital` and `strain` have no Ra basis for carry-over. `doses` and `hp` have no basis in the sources.
