## Energy gateway

**1. Mechanism and rules**

*Mechanism.* A counter-spun toroid pair relaxes toward one lowest-energy state under helicity-conserving dynamics (Taylor relaxation). Relaxation converts stored magnetic energy and never creates it. A level receives energy only through its gateway, an explicit input term.

*Rules.*
1. Total energy is conserved at every step. Each draw is debited one-for-one from the enclosing level. (Ra is silent on conservation; this is a design choice.)
2. A node draws only when its balance exceeds its threshold. (Follows Ra 29.27–29.30.)
3. Nested power comes from the enclosing level. (Departs from Ra 41.4, which names stars as the primary source.)
4. Every draw is logged: source, sink, amount, time.
5. Misused gateways have consequences in the world model. (Follows Ra 22.23, 24.4, 29.31.)
6. A toroid releases only energy it holds or receives through its gateway.

**2. Physical basis**

Fidelity's energy gateway rests on a relaxation model with an established basis in plasma physics. Counter-spun toroidal magnetic fields relax to a single lowest-energy state while conserving magnetic helicity (Taylor relaxation theory). The simulation applies this to toroid pairs in an idealised, axisymmetric, zero-pressure setting. A balanced pair releases about 33% of its stored magnetic energy; a strongly imbalanced pair releases about 96%. The model never creates energy. The gateway is an explicit modelling assumption: energy entering a level is a logged input, debited from the enclosing level.

**3. Default parameters**

- Toroid balance b: 0 (test range 0 to 0.9)
- Reference grid 40×40; 20×20 for quick checks
- Target relaxed state: λ_eff 4.9550; magnetic axis at r = 0.625
- Regression targets (40×40): release 32.86% at b = 0; 96.47% at b = 0.9
- Gateway balance threshold: 0.5 (placeholder; no source value)
- Gateway debit: 1:1, fixed by Rule 1, not tunable
- Draw logging: on
