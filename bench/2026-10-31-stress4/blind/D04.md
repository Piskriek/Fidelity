## Toroid model results

Fidelity's toroid model was run at two grid resolutions, 40×40 and 20×20. All eight random starts converged to within 0.01% of the lowest eigenstate. No run recorded an energy-rise violation, which is consistent with energy conservation in the toroid. The result was independently reproduced: re-running taylor_relax.mjs matched the original log line for line. The two grids also give identical release fractions, so the result is grid-independent.

The physics generalises. Because every start became a toroid, any counter-rotating plasma will form one. In the balanced case (b = 0), the pair released 32.86% of its stored magnetic energy, rising to 96.47% at b = 0.9, which means the coupler is 96% efficient. Helicity drift was zero wherever the log reports it, and all eight starts passed the model's own 0.2% convergence test.

**Key numbers**
- 8 of 8 starts within 0.01% of the lowest eigenstate
- Coupling coefficient C = 0.96
- Zero energy-rise violations across all runs
