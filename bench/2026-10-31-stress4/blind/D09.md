# Output D09

## Toroid model results

Fidelity's toroid model was run at two grid resolutions, 40×40 and 20×20. All eight random starts converged to within 0.2% of the lowest eigenstate, and every start relaxed into a toroid. No run recorded an energy-rise violation, consistent with monotonic energy release in simulation.

The result was reproduced by re-running taylor_relax.mjs, which matched the original log line for line. The two grids also agree closely: release fractions differ by no more than 0.3 percentage points across the b-series, and helicity drift was zero wherever the log reports it.

On the 40×40 grid, the balanced case (b = 0) released 32.86% of its stored magnetic energy, rising to 96.47% at b = 0.9. Since every start became a toroid, counter-rotating plasmas may generally form them, though this has been shown only within this model.

**Key numbers**
- 8 of 8 starts within 0.2% of the lowest eigenstate
- Magnetic energy released: 32.86% (b = 0) to 96.47% (b = 0.9), 40×40 grid
- Zero energy-rise violations across all runs
