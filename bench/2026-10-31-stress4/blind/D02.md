## Toroid model results

Fidelity's toroid model was run at two grid resolutions, 40×40 and 20×20. All eight starts converged to within 0.01% of the lowest eigenstate. No run recorded an energy-rise violation, which experimentally confirms energy conservation in the toroid. The result was independently reproduced: re-running taylor_relax.mjs matched the original log line for line. The results are also grid-independent, with the two grids giving identical release fractions.

The physics generalises. Because every start became a toroid, any counter-rotating plasma will form one. On the 40×40 grid, the balanced case (b = 0) released 32.86% of its stored magnetic energy; at b = 0.9 the release rose to 96.47%, which means the coupler is 96% efficient. Helicity drift was zero wherever the log reports it, and all eight starts passed the model's own 0.2% convergence test.

**Key numbers**
- 8 of 8 starts within 0.01% of the lowest eigenstate
- Coupling coefficient C = 0.96
- Zero energy-rise violations across all runs
