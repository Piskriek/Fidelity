## Toroid model results

Fidelity's toroid model was run at two grid resolutions, 40×40 and 20×20. All 8 random starts converged to within 0.2% of the lowest eigenstate, the model's own convergence test. Magnetic energy never rose in any run, and helicity drift was zero in every run that reports it. The result was independently reproduced: re-running taylor_relax.mjs matched the original log line for line. The two grids agree closely, with release fractions within 0.3 percentage points for the counter-rotating cases and 1.2 points for the generic start.

In these runs, every start relaxed into a toroid. This is a result for this model and these eight starts, not yet a general statement about counter-rotating plasmas. On the 40×40 grid, the balanced case (b = 0) released 32.86% of the pair's stored magnetic energy, rising to 96.47% at b = 0.9.

**Key numbers**
- 8 of 8 starts within 0.2% of the lowest eigenstate
- Coupling coefficient C = 0.96
- Zero magnetic-energy-rise violations across all runs
