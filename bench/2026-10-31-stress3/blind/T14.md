**Toroid model results**

The toroid model was run as a numerical simulation to test how a toroidal plasma relaxes. Every starting configuration tested ended as a toroid, on both a 40×40 and a 20×20 grid. In every run, magnetic energy never rose during relaxation, and magnetic helicity was conserved to within 10⁻⁸. For two stacked counter-poloidal toroids, the simulated energy release matched the approximate Taylor-theory prediction within 0.2 percentage points at every b setting tested.

**Key numbers**

- **96.47%** of magnetic energy released at b = 0.9 on the 40×40 grid. The 20×20 run gives 96.45%, against an approximate Taylor prediction of 96.46%.
- **8 of 8** starts settled to within 0.2% of the analytic lowest eigenvalue (4.9550), on both grids.
- **0.3 points**: largest gap in energy-release fraction between the 40×40 and 20×20 grids, for the stacked-toroid cases.
