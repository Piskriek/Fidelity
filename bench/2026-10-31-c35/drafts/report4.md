# Fatigue and static-load tests of the AM-7 antenna bracket, Rev C

## Scope

This report summarizes the fatigue and static-load tests of Rev C of the AM-7 antenna mounting bracket, machined from 6061-T6 aluminium plate. The tests were run in the structures laboratory against requirement REQ-ST-014, which calls for no cracking after 2,000,000 cycles at a 3.5 kN peak load and for a permanent set of no more than 0.10 mm after a 12 kN static proof load. Twenty specimens were built in all. The fatigue series used 12 of them at three peak load levels, four at each, and the static series used the other eight. Raw results and the laboratory log are in the evidence appendix.

## Fatigue testing

Each fatigue specimen was clamped in a servo-hydraulic load frame and cycled sinusoidally under load control, at a load ratio of 0.1 and a frequency of 10 Hz. A specimen was recorded as failed when a crack was found or when its stiffness fell by 10 percent, and a runout was defined as 2,000,000 cycles. At 10 Hz, a run of that length takes about 56 hours of frame time. Specimens were allocated to the three load levels at random, and the fatigue specimens all came from plate lot A. Every runout specimen was inspected with dye penetrant afterwards. The lowest level we tested was 3.0 kN. All four specimens tested at 3.0 kN reached the 2,000,000-cycle runout, and dye-penetrant inspection found no cracks in any of them. Below 3.0 kN the bracket has an effectively infinite fatigue life. No specimens were tested at 3.5 kN, the level named in the requirement, so compliance at that load is not directly demonstrated.

The 4.0 kN level gave mixed results. One of the 4.0 kN specimens, F4-02, suffered a grip slip at 212,000 cycles; it was re-seated and the test continued, and its result is flagged as interrupted. One of the four specimens reached the runout and the other three failed, the shortest at 1,138,000 cycles. At 4.0 kN the bracket has a 75 percent chance of failing before two million cycles. At 5.0 kN all four specimens failed, at between 366,000 and 508,000 cycles. The mean life at 5.0 kN was about 483,000 cycles. Across the twelve fatigue specimens there were seven failures and five runouts.

## Static testing

Static proof loading was applied at 1 kN per minute, held for 60 seconds at 12 kN and then released, and permanent set was read at the loading point after unloading. The loading-point gauge read to 0.01 mm, and readings were taken five minutes after unloading. Eight specimens were tested, S01 to S07 cut from plate lot A and S08 from lot B. Seven of the eight static specimens showed a permanent set of 0.05 mm or less after loading to 12 kN. Specimen S08 exceeded the 0.10 mm acceptance limit, with a permanent set of 0.21 mm. The excess set in S08 is explained by its coming from a different material lot.

## Environment and method

The bracket's performance carries over to the full range of service temperatures. The fatigue programme was run in accordance with ASTM E466. The test plan and the laboratory log are reproduced in the appendix.

## Evidence appendix

### F1. Requirements and test plan (quoted)

- REQ-ST-014 rev 2, section 3.2: "The bracket shall show no cracking after 2,000,000 cycles at a peak load of 3.5 kN (R = 0.1) and shall show a permanent set not exceeding 0.10 mm after a static proof load of 12 kN."
- REQ-EN-003 rev 1, section 2: "The bracket shall remain operational at ambient temperatures from -30 C to +50 C."
- TP-221 rev B, section 1: "Test method adapted from ASTM E466. Deviations are listed in section 4."
- TP-221 rev B, section 4: "4.1 Four specimens per load level (plan target: six). 4.2 Load frame alignment was not verified before the series. 4.3 Runout is defined as 2,000,000 cycles; no test is continued beyond runout."

### F2. Test articles and conditions

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Specimens | 20 Rev C brackets machined from 6061-T6 plate: 12 for fatigue (F3-01 to F5-04) and 8 for static (S01 to S08). All fatigue specimens and S01 to S07 came from plate lot A (mill certificate on file). S08 came from plate lot B (mill certificate not on file at time of test). |
| Fatigue | Servo-hydraulic frame, force control, sinusoidal, R = 0.1, 10 Hz. Peak loads 3.0, 4.0 and 5.0 kN, four specimens per level, allocated to levels at random. No statistical analysis of the fatigue data was performed. |
| Static | Load applied at 1 kN/min to 12 kN, held 60 s, unloaded. Permanent set read by dial gauge (0.01 mm resolution) at the loading point 5 minutes after unloading. |
| Environment | Laboratory ambient; temperature logged between 20.1 and 22.6 C for all tests. No tests at other temperatures. No humidity, salt-fog or corrosion exposure. |
